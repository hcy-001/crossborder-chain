The enhanced interface also provides clearer suggestions and guidance throughout the listing process.\n\n\n\n\n1. Basic Information\n\nThe Basic information section contains the following components:\n\nProduct Images (Cover Photo is mandatory)\nProduct Video\nProduct Name (mandatory)\nCategory (mandatory)\nProduct Description (mandatory)\n\n\n\n\nProduct Images and Video\n\nUpload a relevant cover photo and up to 8 images to showcase your product. You can also enhance the quality of your listing by uploading a product video to demonstrate how your product works. \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n💡 Tip\n\nListings with multiple high-resolution images that show the product clearly tend to attract more buyers.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nProduct Name and Category\n\nHaving a relevant product name and the right category ensures shoppers can find your product easily during search. Enter a product name and select the appropriate category. You may see category recommendations based on the product images and name you've provided.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n💡 Tip\nExplore Shopee's product categories and to find out which category your product belongs to.\nIf you're not sure how to best name your product, try this naming convention: brand + specifications and type + model \nAvoid ALL CAPS, unnecessary symbols, and keyword spams\n\n\n\n\nProduct Description \n\nProviding a detailed product description reduces the need for enquiry and can help shoppers make more confident purchases. You can also highlight your product's best selling points and benefits here.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n💡 Tip\n\nTo create an informative product description, you should include the product's technical specifications, unique features, and warranty information (if applicable). Learn more about improving the quality of your product listing.\n\n\n\n\nSelected sellers can also upload images as part of their product description to create a more engaging and informative experience for shoppers. If this applies to you, you can upload up to 12 images via your local drive or the Image Creator tool.\n\n\n\n\n⚠️ Note\n\nWith Smart Template, Product Name, Product Category and Description will be pre-filled/ recommended when a Product Image is uploaded.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n2. Specification\n\nYou are required to fill in mandatory product attributes, such as Brand or Material, in the Specification section to help shoppers understand your product better. Do fill in the mandatory product attributes that are marked by an asterisk (*) so that you can upload your listing successfully.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nThe product attribute information required is dependent on the product’s category. Learn more about product categories and attributes.\n\n\n\n\n⚠️ Note\n\nWith Smart Template, selected fields in the Specification tab will be pre-filled / recommended when a Product Image is uploaded. \n\n\n\n\n﻿\n\n3. Sales Information\n\nThe Sales Information section contains the following components:\n\nVariations (optional)\nPrice (mandatory)\nStock (mandatory)\nWholesale (optional) \nSize Chart (optional)\n\n\n\n\nVariations\n\nAdding product variations, such as size and colour, lets shoppers select different options within a single product listing. Each product can have up to 2 types of variations. \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nIf there are different variations for your product, select Enable Variations.\n\n\n\n\nFill in the variation Name and Options, followed by Price, Stock, and SKU Number (optional) for each variation option. You can also add an image for each option.\n\n\n\n\nIf your product has 2 types of variations, select + Add Variation 2 to set up your second  variation type.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n💡  Increase your products' searchability!\n\n\n\n\nWhen you select Enable Variations or click on an input box, a dropdown menu may appear. This menu contains a list of standard variation names commonly input by sellers on Shopee. Choosing a name from this menu will help your product appear in more searches.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n💡 Tip\n\nWhile it is optional to add images for your product variation, it can help shoppers to visualise and select available options easily.  \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nPrice \n\nSet a competitive price to make your product more attractive. Find out the market price of your product by comparing against similar products on Shopee or other platforms.\n\n\n\n\nStock\n\nEnsure you provide an accurate stock number by checking against the actual stock in your warehouse or inventory.\n\n\n\n\nWholesale\n\nFor products that tend to be bought in larger quantities, consider setting wholesale prices to encourage shoppers to buy more. If your product has variations, ensure that all variations have the same price before you set a wholesale price.\n\n\n\n\nTo set a wholesale price, select Add Price Tier and fill in the minimum and maximum quantities applicable. The Unit Price is the price of a single product in the tier, which will be applied when a buyer meets the quantity requirement. \n\n\n\n\n⚠️ Note\nYou can set up to 5 tiers of wholesale prices.\nThe unit price of each tier must always be lower than that of the previous tier (e.g. if Tier 1 is $5, Tier 2 must be cheaper than $5). \nLearn more about wholesale prices.\n\n\n\n\nSize Chart\n\nIf applicable, upload a size chart for your product to help shoppers find the best fit during their purchase. Providing accurate measurements can help reduce potential return/refund.\n\n\n\n\n4. Shipping Information\n\nThe Shipping section contains 3 components:\n\nWeight (kg) (mandatory)\nParcel Size (optional)\nShipping Fee (mandatory)\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nWeight and Parcel Size\n\nFill in an accurate parcel weight (inclusive of product and packaging) and parcel  dimensions to ensure that shipping fees are calculated correctly. \n\n\n\n\nProducts with variations that are in different weights or sizes may have different shipping fees. To ensure that shipping fees are accurately and fairly calculated, turn on the switch for Different weight/dimensions for variations and enter the weight and dimensions of each variation separately.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nShipping Fee\n\nEnable at least 1 shipping channel from the list of options available. These options are based on the shipping channels you have enabled for your shop via the Shipping Setting page. Your shipping fee will be calculated based on the parcel weight and dimensions provided. \n\n\n\n\n5. Other Information\n\nUnder Others, indicate if your product is a Pre-Order. Fill in additional information, such as the condition of your product. Select Save and Publish to upload your product listing.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nEnabling Affiliate Marketing Solutions\n\n\n\n\nUpon listing your product, you can opt to enable Affiliate Marketing Solutions (AMS) for your product to boost sales.\n\n\n\n\nYou can specify the percentage of sales the Affiliate will receive for successful sales by adjusting the Affiliate Commission Rate.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nYou may also enroll your product for AMS on the My Products page by selecting More under Actions and then selecting Set Affiliate Commission.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nTo remove an AMS Commission Rate or opt your product out of AMS, please visit the Affiliate Marketing page.\n\n\n\n\nSchedule Publish Time\n\n\n\n\nUse the Schedule Publish Time function if you wish to upload your product at a later time or date.\n\n\n\n\nThe earliest you can schedule a product to be published is an hour from the time of scheduling. You can schedule products to be published up to 90 days in advance.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nSelect Save and Delist at the bottom of the page to schedule your product listing.\n\n\n\n\nTo view your scheduled product listings, go to My Products and open the Product Label menu. Then, select Scheduled Products\n\n\n\n\nListing a product on Shopee App\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nTo upload your product listing on Shopee App, go to the Me tab. Filling in basic information\n\n\n\n\nEnter an appropriate Product Name as well as the Product Description for your listing in the respective fields. Choosing the right categories\n\n\n\n\nSelect Category to choose the appropriate category for your product. If you are unsure which category your product belongs to, explore the list of Shopee's product categories to find out.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nYou can also search for your product's category by selecting the Search icon on the top right corner and entering keywords related to your product. \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n4. Filling in product specifications\n\n\n\n\nProvide mandatory product attributes by filling in the fields that are marked by an asterisk (*). The fields you are required to fill in are dependent on your product's category. \n\n\n\n\nProviding useful information, such as the Brand and Pattern of your product, can help shoppers understand your product better.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n5. Filling in sales information\n\n\n\n\nSelect Variations to add product variations, such as colour and size, to allow shoppers to select different options within your product listing. \n\n\n\n\nAdding variations\n\nOpen the Variations menu. For selected categories, a list of suggested variation types and options may be displayed. Choosing a name from this menu will help your product appear in more searches.\n\n\n\n\nTo manually input a variation type, select Add Custom Variations. Select + Add under Self-fill Option to input a variation option if it is not on the suggested list. \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nEditing and deleting variations\n\nEach product can have up to 2 variation types. After setting up the first variation type, you can add a second variation type by selecting Add Variation.\n\n\n\n\nRemove a variation type and all options under the variation type by selecting Delete, or remove individual options under a variation type by selecting the 'x' icon beside the option.\n\n\n\n\nYou may rename a variation type or option by selecting the edit icon next to its name.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nAdding images to a variation\n\nTo add an image specific to a variation that is different from your main product image, select an image icon beside a variation option. For products with 2 variation types, images can only be added to the variation type that you have added first.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nTo delete or edit variation images, select the image you have uploaded and choose the action you wish to perform in the pop up menu.\n\n\n\n\nAdding variation information\n\nSelect Next: Set Variation Info to add the Price and Stock information for each type of variation. \n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nYou can also choose to set the same price and stock for all types of variations by selecting Batch Edit. Then, proceed to save the changes once you are done.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nThe Price and Stock fields on the Add Product page will be automatically filled in. If you did not add any product variations, ensure you fill in these fields separately.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nSetting wholesale price\n\nSelect Wholesale to set wholesale prices and encourage shoppers to buy more. Enter the Min Order and Max Order applicable, followed by the Unit Price. You can also add more price tiers by selecting Add Price Tier. \n\n\n\n\nIf your product has variations, ensure that all variations have the same price before you set a wholesale price.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n6. Filling in shipping information\n\n\n\n\nSelect Shipping fee to fill in your parcel weight (inclusive of product and packaging) and dimensions (if applicable). Then, enable at least 1 shipping channel as a shipping option for this product, so that you can successfully list it.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\nProducts with variations that are in different weights or sizes may have different shipping fees. To ensure that shipping fees are accurately and fairly calculated, turn on the switch for Different weight/dimensions for variations and enter the weight and dimensions of each variation separately.\n\n\n\n\n\n\n\n7. Filling in additional information\n\n\n\n\nFill in additional information, such as the Condition of your product and indicate if your product is a Pre-Order. You can also share your product listing on social media platforms such as Twitter and Facebook by enabling the relevant toggle.
