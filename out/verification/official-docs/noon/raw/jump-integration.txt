At noon, we aim to develop the seller and provide them with a platform to support order fulfillment. In today's world of quick commerce and E-commerce, the seller's ability to quickly fulfill orders is a crucial aspect of the business. JUMP is a platform that acts as a bridge between your external store, such as the SALLA , and the noon fulfillment services. You can very easily get onboarded with JUMP by fulfilling 3 simple steps. Creating a JUMP account on Seller Lab. Linking your Legal entity & Signing the Contract Integrating your noon account with SALLA Store. Creating a JUMP account on the Seller Lab. You must create a JUMP account to get started with the onboarding process. To do so, you must follow these simple steps: - Login to the website https://welcome.noon.partners/ using your noon credentials. Note: You need to have a noon account first to be able to get onboarded on JUMP. If you don't have a noon account, you need to create one. Refer to this article to know the process Specifically, follow Step 1 to create your Organization ID and the first part of Step 2 to complete your store creation —this includes filling in your store details , display name , and business information . Once your store is created, return here to continue the JUMP setup. - On the welcome page, click on Create under the JUMP section. - You will be requested to provide the details such as the Country, Phone number, Email ID, and the Store name. Then click on Create Account button to create your JUMP store. Note: JUMP services is only available for the KSA seller for now. - You will be redirected to the JUMP store, and you will be able to view the details of your store. Linking the Legal Entity and Signing the Contract. The next step in JUMP onboarding it to link your Legal Entity and sign the Contract. Info: If you already have a Legal Entity created and approved on noon, you do not have to create another Legal Entity separately. However, if you wish to create a separate Legal entity for JUMP, you can do that. Refer to this article . - Start by clicking on the drop-down under the Legal entity section to select an already existing Legal Entity or click on the Create New Legal entity button to create a new one. - Once you select the Legal entity, you will notice a blue button called Sign Contract. - You will be redirected to the Authorized Signatory section to select the person who will sign the contract. Select from the drop-down and click on the Send T&C for Review button. You will receive an email associated with the signatory, and you need to sign the contract to agree to the Terms and Conditions. - You will get an email with the Terms and Conditions. Click on Review/Sign Contract. - You need to acknowledge the disclaimer and agree to the Terms and Conditions. - This completes the steps of linking the legal entity and signing the contract. - In the JUMP store, you will also find an option to link a bank account for the payments. You can select the bank account from the drop-down and click on Link Bank account. - Once the bank account is linked, it will appear as shown in the screenshot below. Integrating your noon account with the SALLA store This brings us to the final step in your JUMP onboarding process, which is to integrate your noon account with your SALLA store. - To proceed with the integration, you will find the integration and settings section on your JUMP account. Click on the blue button called Integrate Channel. - In the next step, you will see the option to connect your SALLA store. Click on the Connect button. - In the next step, you will be requested to authorize JUMP on your SALLA account. You will have two options. 1. Fulfilled by Jump - Jump will handle the full fulfillment & Logistics Cycle. You can store your products in our warehouses, manage your catalog through JUMP Catalog and get packing & shipping Services 2. Delivered by Jump - Ship through our JUMP Services while handling the fulfillment & warehousing from your end. Note: If you choose the preference to Fulfill by Jump you will be requested to complete 2 steps - Create your Catalog : You will be requested to create the catalog for your SKUs. Refer to this article for more information The most important fields to be filled would be as mentioned below : Name Description Image SKU ( That matched your salla store SKU ) Barcode - Transferring the stock to noon wareshouse: You need to transfer the stock by creating an ASN to the noon warehouse. Refer to this article for more information. - Select the service you want to work with, then click Authorize Jump on Salla . - You will be redirected to the SALLA platform. You need to log in to your account by providing the credentials of your SALLA store. - Once you log in, you will see a button Granting the permit . Click on this button. - Your request will be processed, and you will be redirected to the JUMP account on Seller Lab, showing a success message that the SALLA account is connected. This completes the integration process. Other functionalities under Integrating Channel Once your SALLA account is connected, the Integration and Settings option will offer new actions that you can take on the JUMP account. Let's discuss these functions in brief. - To access these actions, click on the blue Integrate channel button. - This will redirect you to the JUMP Account settings. Here you can view the Store details and the Linked channel, which is your SALLA store. Click on the Channel Preference button. - Here you will have the option to visit the channel on the SALLA platform. You will have another option to deactivate the channel . This section will also allow you to select your order preference. Once your order preference is selected, you can click on Save Changes to update the preference. - Once the preference is updated, you will get a confirmation message that your preferences have been updated successfully.
