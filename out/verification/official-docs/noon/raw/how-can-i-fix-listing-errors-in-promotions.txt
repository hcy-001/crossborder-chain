When you list products in promotions and submit them for review, noon will either approve your submission or reject it. You'll find the rejection reason next to each product. Simply update your product according to the rejection reason and resubmit it for approval. This article explains the listing rejection reasons, how to fix errors, and how to resubmit your product for approval. Why did my products fail the content quality check? Deal inactive The deal isn't active during a specific period of time Select another deal that is available at this time or in the near future.
