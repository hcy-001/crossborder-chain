In order to ensure a proper understanding of your product to the customer, please follow the structure of the document to create content. You can begin with: Title creation. Image upload. Listing product features and attributes. A few points to note: Be precise in your text. Upload high quality images. Avoid repetition of content/images. Highlight the unique aspects of the product. Please remember this is your only way of communicating to your customer, make the most of it Title Guidelines The title is one of the most prominent and important parts of the product listing and a key factor for ensuring a positive customer experience. A specific and accurate product title can drive valuable leads to the listing, on the other hand poorly structured titles can have a negative impact on customer’s buying experience. Good images make it easy for customers to evaluate the product before even they refer to the product information. Therefore, it is highly recommended to upload a minimum of 3 high-quality images for your product displaying different alternate angles, sizes, textures, etc. for an easy understanding of the product for the customer. Any other angles Feature Guideline Feature Bullets (Product Highlights) is one of the important sections where you can describe the selling features of the product in detail. If there is any important information that cannot be mentioned in attributes for some reason, has to be captured in any one of the highlights. It is one of the important elements of the product page as it draws attention to the important information which makes the customers’ purchase decision easier. Therefore, It is recommended to write 5 feature bullets per product and ensure that those focus on the key USP of the product.
