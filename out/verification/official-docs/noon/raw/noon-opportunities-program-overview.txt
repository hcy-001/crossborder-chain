noon Opportunities - Program Overview | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Product Listing › noon Opportunities - Program Overview noon Opportunities - Program Overview noon Opportunities (Assortment Opportunity) allows sellers to add high-demand products directly to their catalog. Since noon provides pre-approved content (titles, descriptions, images, and no Quality Control required), you can quickly list these SKUs provided by noon and make them live by setting your price and inventory details. Note : This feature is only available for selected sellers for now, and the selection is based on the invitation from the commercial team. Program Eligibility & Scope Where is this feature live currently : Available for the United Arab Emirates (UAE) market only. Fulfillment Channels : This is eligible for both Fulfilled by noon (FBN) and Directship Express (DS Express) only. Account Specific : Benefits apply strictly to the specific SKUs offered to your account under the program and cannot be transferred to another account or SKU. New SKUs Only : The product must be completely new to your account. Relisting a product you already sell on noon does not qualify. Program Benefits 100% Commission Waiver: 100% of the commission is waived for 60 days, starting from the exact date the SKU first goes live on your account. This benefit applies to both FBN and DS Express orders. Waived FBN Storage Fees: FBN storage fees are waived for 6 months, starting from the date stock is first received into a noon fulfillment center. Note : Storage fee waivers apply to FBN only (DS Express does not incur noon storage fees). Continuous Benefit Periods: Both the benefits mentioned above run continuously from their initial start dates. Periods will not be paused or extended if the SKU goes out of stock, and subsequent inventory inbounds will not reset the 6-month storage timer. Key conditions to maintain benefits To remain eligible for program benefits, you must meet the following criteria: Express Orders Only: The commission waiver applies exclusively to orders fulfilled via Express. Non-express orders of the same SKU will not qualify. Product should be an exact match : You can use the listing provided by noon opportunities on Seller Lab, but the physical item shipped must also match exactly what noon specified (no substitutes, different variants, alternate pack sizes, or quantity differences). If a product differs, eligibility will be revoked immediately, and past refunds may be reclaimed. Price Range Compliance: Each SKU carries a designated Minimum and Maximum price set by noon. Units sold outside this range will not qualify for commission waivers (evaluated based on the item's price at the time the order is placed). Keeping the product in stock: You must keep the SKU active, live, and continuously available on Express. Payment & Refund Process Charged at sale, refunded later: Commission is charged as normal at the point of sale. noon then refunds 100% of the charged commission back to your seller account (it is processed as a cash refund, not a point-of-sale discount or store credit). How does the payment work : Refunds are paid out by the end of the calendar month in which the order is delivered. If an order is placed in one month but delivered in the following month, the refund will be credited in the month of delivery. Delivered orders only: Only successfully delivered orders qualify. Cancelled, undelivered, returned, or Return-to-Origin (RTO) orders do not qualify. Withholding & Recovery Policy noon reserves the right to withhold refunds or reclaim previously refunded amounts if: An order is later cancelled, returned, or not delivered. Any program conditions (pricing, product matching, Express fulfillment) are violated. noon detects fraud, self-purchasing, counterfeit items, or items that do not match the listing. Note : In cases of identified fraud, noon reserves full legal and operational rights under the Seller Agreement, including withholding all account refunds and removing the SKUs. Step-by-Step Guide Step 1: Access noon Opportunities and browse / filter opportunities Log in to your noon Seller Lab . On the left navigation menu under Catalog, click Opportunities . Please note: You will see this option in the navigation bar only if you are invited to be a part of this program. You will see two main tabs at the top: Open Opportunities: Products available for you to add. Seized Opportunities: Products you have already added to your catalog. Under Open Opportunities , browse the list of products. Use the search bar to find specific products by keyword or SKU. Use filters on the right (e.g., Brand or Product Family ) to narrow down your results. Step 2 : Add Products to Your Catalog Locate the product you want to sell. Review the Suggested Price provided under the Price column for pricing guidance. Click the Add to my catalog button under the Action column. The system will display Adding... followed by Added to your catalog . Note: Once the SKU is added to your catalog, a hyperlink will appear directly in the Action column in place of the button. Clicking this hyperlink will immediately redirect you to the Content page of that SKU, where you can review pre-approved details (Basic Content, Images, Videos, etc.). Step 3: Configure Pricing and Stock (Go Live) Go to the Seized Opportunities tab to locate all added products. Click on the product name or hyperlinked SKU to open its product page. Under the Offer tab, enter your offer parameters: Base Price / Final Price: Enter your selling price. Stock Information: Select FBN or FBP warehouse stock arrangements. Barcode & Warranty Details: Fill in relevant information if required. Note: If these SKUs go offline due to a stock or price issue, you can manage them under the Seized Opportunities tab as well. Under the Content tab, verify that English/Arabic content status displays QC Accepted . Click Save Changes at the top right to complete your listing. That’s it! Got questions?
