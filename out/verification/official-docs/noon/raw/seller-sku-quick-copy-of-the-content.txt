The Quick Copy Content feature in Seller Lab allows you to easily create multiple similar products by duplicating an existing Seller SKU’s content. This is particularly useful for creating variations, such as products in different colors, without manually re-entering all the details. You can quickly copy content from one SKU to another, adjust attributes as needed, and publish your new product directly in Seller Lab. This allows you to copy content directly in Seller Lab instead of using the bulk template. Steps to Use the Quick Copy Content Feature To use this feature follow below steps: 1. Find the Product to Add Content : Go to Catalog > My Catalog> search for the SKU and click on the PSKU to begin filling in the content. 2. Select the SKU : Select the SKU that you want to copy. 3. Visit the Content Tab : In the Content page, click on the Copy product content button. 4. Select the SKU you want to copy from : Choose the SKU you wish to duplicate, then click on Select Product. Then select the attributes you want to copy in both (e.g., English and Arabic) languages. Ensure you scroll through all attributes to include everything you need. Click on Save to apply the copied information to your new SKU. That's it! Got more questions? Contact us at seller@noon.com
