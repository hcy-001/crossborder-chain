When you’re creating your Seller SKU products it’s important to know what language your content has to be in. To adjust to the needs of the different markets, the requirements are different depending on what country you’re selling your products in. Is noon providing the translation for the content of the Seller SKU? We are not providing translations for the Seller SKU products. Every seller is responsible to create the content in both languages if needed. What is the required language for each country on noon website? If you only provide one language content the visibility of your SKU might be limited . For example, if you only provide English content for a product being sold on the UAE website, that product might only get shown on the English website and be hidden from the Arabic one. Country English Arabic UAE Mandatory Optional KSA Mandatory Optional EGY Mandatory if no Arabic Mandatory if no English United Arab Emirates All of your Seller SKUs have to have English content. This means that your English content has to be approved for your product to be eligible to go live. Arabic content is optional. It’s advisable for you to at least provide the Arabic title, if not the other content. Kingdom of Saudi Arabia All of your Seller SKUs have to have English content. This means that your English content has to be approved for your product to be eligible to go live. Arabic content is optional. It’s advisable for you to at least provide the Arabic title, if not the other content. Arab Republic of Egypt Either your Arabic or English content have to be approved for your SKU to be eligible to go live. That's it! Got more questions? Contact us at seller@noon.com
