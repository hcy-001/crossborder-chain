Trust & safety: How to use the noon whistleblowing hotline | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Getting Help & Support › Trust & safety: How to use the noon whistleblowing hotline Trust & safety: How to use the noon whistleblowing hotline At noon, we are committed to building a fair, transparent, and trustworthy marketplace for everyone. To protect your business and maintain platform integrity, we provide a safe, confidential Whistleblowing Hotline to report serious misconduct or unethical behavior. What should I report? This channel is reserved exclusively for serious misconduct . You should speak up if you encounter or suspect: Fraud & Corruption: Actual or suspected operational or financial fraud, blackmail, or conflicts of interest. Bribery & Kickbacks: Anyone requesting payments, gifts, or favors. Data & Security Breaches: Unauthorized access to your account/data, or improper sharing of your pricing and business details. Unfair Treatment: Procurement misconduct or unfair vendor/seller treatment. Policy Violations: Serious infractions impacting sellers, partners, or customers. Note: This channel is NOT for routine operational issues like listing errors, payment updates, or general account queries. For these everyday matters, please contact Seller Support directly. Who can be reported? Misconduct can come from any direction. You can report anyone violating our marketplace ethics, including: Who Examples of reportable behavior noon Staff / Employees Asking for bribes/favors to approve listings, manipulate rankings, or speed up account actions. Logistics & pickup drivers Demanding bribes to collect your items or intentionally mishandling shipments. Other Sellers / Suppliers Engaging in price-fixing, fake reviews, or unfair competitive practices. Customers Organized return fraud or scam purchase patterns. Your own representatives An agent acting on your behalf who commits fraud against noon. Your protections & peace of mind. We take your safety seriously. When you submit a report in good faith, noon guarantees: Confidentiality: Your identity is protected to the absolute fullest extent possible. Anonymity: You can choose to file your report entirely anonymously without revealing who you are. Zero Retaliation: Good-faith reporting will never result in unfair treatment, retaliation, or adverse action against your noon seller account. Please note: Intentionally false or malicious reports are not protected and may result in platform action against the reporter. How to submit a report? When filing a report, please include as much detail as possible—what happened, when it occurred, who was involved, and any supporting evidence (like screenshots or messages) you have.
