Understanding the Directship Outbound Fee The Directship Outbound Fee is charged at either delivered_date or Return_date (whatever comes first). It is calculated on the higher of the shipment’s physical or volumetric weight. Note that this fee is non-refundable, except in cases like customer unresponsiveness leading to undelivered shipments. Calculating the Directship Outbound Fee Step 1: Classification and Weight Calculation To calculate the fee: Select your shipment’s classification (standard or oversize) based on its dimensions. Calculate both the shipping weight (physical weight + packaging weight) and volumetric weight (Length x Breadth x Height / 5000), rounding up to the nearest 50g. Use the higher of these two weights as the base for your fee calculation. Ensure the weight doesn’t exceed the maximum limit for its classification. The corresponding shipping weight is: Shipping Weight = Shipment's physical weight + Packaging Weight = (0.32+0.10) kg = 0.42 kg (approximately 0.5 kg) Volumetric Weight : Length*Breadth*Height/5000 = 17.4*8.81*2.8/5000= 0.086 kg. Since shipping weight is higher we take that as the base. Therefore, this is under the 500 g maximum shipping weight for standard parcel.
