Yes, if you are a seller registered in China with a valid Chinese Commercial Registration you are eligible to list and sell under noon Global Shipping model What is the pricing structure for the 'noon Global Shipping' model? The noon Global Shipping model is designed to make international selling simple and hassle-free for sellers. In this model, the seller uploads the price in USD of a product (called Transfer Price in noon’s system). This is the amount the seller expects to receive for each unit sold. The ‘Transfer Price’ should exclude all international logistics-related costs such as cross-border shipping, customs duties, taxes, and clearance fees, as these are fully handled by noon. noon will cover the costs related to international logistics, customs duties, clearance, and other cross-border operational expenses and will be charged to the customer directly By doing this, sellers do not need to calculate or manage complex international shipping or customs costs. noon determines the markup internally and takes full ownership of cross-border logistics, allowing sellers to focus only on setting a competitive Transfer Price and growing their business. Who is responsible for VAT in destination countries? noon calculates and pays the applicable VAT to the respective tax authorities based on the prevailing tax rates in each destination market. Sellers are not required to register for VAT or manage VAT payments in the destination countries How does the payout work? Under the noon Global Shipping model, noon pays the seller once an item is successfully delivered to the customer. The payout amount is equal to the Transfer Price set by the seller for the product. There are no additional fees or charges deducted from this amount. Sellers are paid strictly based on the Transfer Price they have defined. Payouts follow noon’s regular Payment Processing Policy Note: A valid payment method is required for new Chinese sellers before taking their store live. Existing sellers who are already live without a payment method will remain active, but adding a payment method is strongly recommended to prevent future disruptions. In case of returns, sellers don’t have to worry about shipping it back to China - noon will take care of it by assessing the salability of the returned item and handling the next steps: Scenario 1: Item is salable noon will list the items as local FBN inventory Once it is listed under FBN, the items will follow the FBN rate card for the marketplace (Refer to the AE and SA FBN Rate cards for more details Sellers are encouraged to set the product price directly through Seller Lab. If a price is not added, noon will automatically apply a default price to ensure your product goes live without any delays. This default price is calculated to make sure you receive an amount close to your Transfer Price at the time of sale. You can modify or update the price anytime from Seller Lab noon will also waive off the FBN storage fee for up to 60 days Scenario 2: Item is non-salable (in case of customer returns) - noon will support in liquidating the items in UAE/KSA. In this case, noon will not charge any inventory removal or disposal fees to the seller. However, the seller will not receive any proceeds from these items, or alternatively, the seller may choose to return them to their local warehouse (RTV).
