FBN warehouses in Egypt for over 6 months . To avoid these charges, it is essential to retrieve aging stock before the respective time limit. The Saleable Recommended RTV Report helps sellers identify such stock, simplifying the retrieval process and preventing storage fees. Below is a guide on how to access this report and an explanation of the included data. How to access this report? Accessing this report is a very easy and simple procedure. All you have to do it follow the simple steps given below. 1. Click on Navigation menu , then select Fulfilled by noon and choose the Reports. 2. Click on Generate Report . 3. Under Report Name , select a category and choose Returns .From the Returns Reports tab, select Saleable Recommended RTV Report. Choose a Close Date and then click on Generate Report to complete the process.
