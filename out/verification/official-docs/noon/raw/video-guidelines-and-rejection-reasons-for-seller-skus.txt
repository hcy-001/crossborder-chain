Video guidelines and rejection reasons for seller SKUs | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Product Listing › Video guidelines and rejection reasons for seller SKUs Video guidelines and rejection reasons for seller SKUs High-quality, informative product videos are an effective way to enhance your listings and drive sales on noon. Videos give customers a clearer understanding of your product’s features, benefits, and usability. However, to maintain a safe and consistent shopping experience, all videos must comply with noon’s content standards and publishing rules. This guide outlines the mandatory requirements and key recommendations to ensure your videos meet our platform guidelines and avoid rejections. Note: Video upload is currently enabled for a limited group of sellers. If you don’t see the Add video button in Seller Lab, it means your account hasn’t been activated yet. We’re rolling this feature out in phases and expect it to be available to all sellers soon. How to Upload a Product Video if “Add video” button is available To upload the infographic content to noon, follow these simple steps - Click on Catalog . Then select My Catalog . - Select the SKU you want to update the video for. - Click on Content Tab . Then click on Additional Media. - Upload your video while ensuring it meets all the technical and content guidelines mentioned in this article. How to Upload a Product Video if “Add video” button is NOT available Review the A+ / PDP Video Guidelines mentioned below. Prepare your video in the required format. Open a Seller Support ticket : Subject: “Video Upload Request – SKU XXXXXXXXX” Attach the video file(s). Our catalog team will upload the video and confirm once it is live. Turn-around time: Typically 1 – 2 business days once the file passes QC. Essential Video Requirements The following standards must be met for your product videos to be accepted on noon: Requirement Guideline Language Videos must be in English or Arabic only. Audio Quality Audio must be clear and free from background noise . Music Use only royalty-free or licensed background music . Branding Only one brand logo may be shown per video view. Product Focus The video should focus solely on the product , highlighting its features, benefits, and usage. Product Variations If showing multiple variants (e.g., color or size), each should be clearly identified to avoid customer confusion. Demonstrations Include accurate and clear product demonstrations where applicable (e.g., how to use, assemble, or install). Endorsements & Testimonials Must be truthful, non-misleading , and comply with local advertising regulations. Environmental Claims Any claims such as “eco-friendly” or “biodegradable” must be backed by credible evidence . Compliance Videos must comply with local laws , advertising standards , and consumer protection laws . Cultural Sensitivity Avoid content that may be considered offensive or inappropriate across noon’s operating regions. Children in Videos Videos must not be directed at children . If minors are included, it must be in context to the product (e.g., toys or children’s clothing). Video Format & Technical Specifications To ensure successful upload and playback, your videos must meet the following technical requirements: Specification Requirement Accepted File Formats MP4, MOV, WEBM ( MP4 recommended ) Resolution 1080p+ recommended , minimum 480p Maximum File Size 5 GB ( Optimal: 10 MB ) Maximum Duration 2.5 minutes ( Optimal: 30 seconds ) Video Content Restrictions 1- Prohibited Content The following types of content are strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate rejection: Nudity or sexually suggestive visuals False, deceptive, or misleading information Derogatory remarks about other brands or competitors Use of copyrighted content (music, images, video) without permission Sharing personal data (names, phone numbers, emails) Content that is emotionally distressing, controversial, or shocking Discriminatory or offensive language Profanity, vulgarity, or defamatory content. Promotion of illegal, unsafe, or violent behavior Any implication of criminal use of the product Swimwear or underwear modeled by minors 2- Promotional & Commercial Restrictions Avoid including marketing-driven or time-sensitive language that can become outdated or misleading: Not Allowed Examples Pricing & Discounts “On Sale Now”, “Best Price”, “Limited Time Offer” Shipping Info “Free Shipping”, “Delivered in 24 Hours” Customer Reviews "Top-rated on noon", "5-star feedback from our customers" Guarantees & Warranties “Money Back Guarantee”, “100% Satisfaction” Competitor Comparisons "Better than [brand]", "product only sold by authorized sellers" 3- Branding and External References To protect brand integrity and prevent redirection away from noon, the following are not allowed: Seller contact details (email, phone number, business name) External web links, QR codes, or URLs Competitor comparisons or unauthorized “official seller” claims Multiple brand logos on screen at once (limit to one per video) 4- Children in Videos Sellers must be extra cautious when featuring minors: Videos must not be directed at children Minors can only appear if directly related to the product No nudity or sexually suggestive content is permitted, including modeling swimwear or undergarments 5- Health and Safety Claims Product videos must avoid unverified medical or health claims, especially for health-related products. Ensure the following: Do not make claims like “cures headaches” or “heals skin conditions” Do not promote weight loss without stating “along with a healthy diet and exercise” Avoid presenting your product as a substitute for medical advice or treatment Video Rejection Reasons Rejection Code/ reason Description Example banned_products_sexual The video contains sexually suggestive or inappropriate imagery. Content that includes nudity, suggestive poses, focus on intimate body parts, or adults modeling children’s underwear inappropriately is strictly prohibited. inappropriate_offensive_language The video contains vulgar, offensive, or discriminatory language. Content with profanity, hate speech, discrimination, inappropriate jokes, or offensive background music is not allowed. inappropriate_obscene_video The video includes disturbing, violent, or obscene footage. Videos containing graphic violence, obscene gestures, disturbing visuals, cruelty, or unsafe activities without warnings are strictly prohibited. inconsistent_video_content The video content does not match the product information Videos showing a different product model, incorrect features, mismatched colors or sizes, non-included accessories, unsupported uses, or incorrect branding are not accepted. invalid_video_content The video includes content not permitted by noon’s guideline Videos containing external links, contact details, customer reviews, social media info, time-sensitive promos, calls to action, warranty/shipping details, or audio in languages other than English or Arabic are prohibited. banned_products_dangerous The video features dangerous or restricted products. Videos featuring weapons, security bypass tools, illegal activities, hazardous materials without warnings, or banned/non-certified products are not allowed. banned_products_copyright The video uses copyrighted material without permission. Videos using unauthorized music, film clips, protected artwork, copyrighted characters, or unlicensed content are prohibited. banned_products_food_drugs_animals The video promotes health products with medical claims or features prohibited items. Videos promoting unregulated medications, making medical claims, or showing health improvement testimonials are not allowed. Best Practices for Approval Keep your message simple , relevant , and product-focused Use high-quality visuals and clear audio Highlight key features and demonstrate real-life usage Ensure all claims are truthful and verifiable Speak clearly or use readable on-screen text That's it!
