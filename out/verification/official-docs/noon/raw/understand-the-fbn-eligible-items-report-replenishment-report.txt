The FBN Eligible Items Report is a powerful, real-time optimization tool that helps you identify which SKUs to replenish, assess future demand, and determine recommended restocking quantities based on historical sales data. Important Note: Use this report to create FBN ASNs that will be approved instantly and speed up your stock replenishment. Why Use the FBN Eligible Items Report? The FBN Eligible Items Report offers several benefits to help you optimize your inventory management: Maintain Optimal Stock Levels: Keep the right quantities in stock to avoid being out of stock. Prevent overstocking, tied-up capital, and unnecessary long-term storage fees. Demand-Based Replenishment: The report uses historical sales data to predict customer demand and recommend quantities to replenish. Accelerated Stock Transfers: Use this report to create FBN ASNs (Advanced Shipping Notices) that are instantly approved, speeding up the stock replenishment process. Accessing the FBN Eligible Items Report - Click on Fulfilled by noon, then click on My ASN & Storage. - Then click on Create ASN. - Click on the Upload CSV file option. - Then click on Download Now or click on Update & Download to download the FBN Eligible Report.
