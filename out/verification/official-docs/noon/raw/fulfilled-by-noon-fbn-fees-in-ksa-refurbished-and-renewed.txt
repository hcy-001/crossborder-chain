These fees include long-term storage, non-saleable item storage, warranty claims, miscellaneous irregularities, and retention penalties for non-compliance with local laws and policies. 1. Long-term storage A fee applicable to each item stored in a noon fulfillment center in the aging bucket of more than 365 days. Warranty Fees (WNTY) A charge of warranty claims made by the customers which the seller couldn't fulfill as per the local laws and noon's policies. 4. Technician Inspection Failure Fee Note : This fee will be effective from 1st November 2026. To maintain high-quality standards for Renewed & Refurbished products sold via Fulfilled by noon (FBN) , inbound shipments undergo a technical inspection upon arrival at the fulfillment center. For this reason, FBN sellers will incur a Technician Inspection Failure Fee only when a unit is rejected during inspection due to issues like functional defects, while inventory that passes the inspection will incur no charge. KSA rate: 1% of the ASP per unit of the last 30 days or 16 SAR, whichever is higher. 5. Retention Penalty fees (RETP) The Retention Penalty Fee (RETP) is charged to the seller to compensate our customers for grievances arising from non-adherence to local laws and our policies. These grievances can be due to, but are not limited to, items that are missing parts/accessories, counterfeit, damaged, defective, used, expired, not matching the product listing, or having pricing errors. 6. Miscellaneous Fees (MISC) As amount charged to the seller for any irregular occurrences that they may cause, which engender grievances to our customers, or/ and are against the local laws and noon policies, such as counterfeit penalties.
