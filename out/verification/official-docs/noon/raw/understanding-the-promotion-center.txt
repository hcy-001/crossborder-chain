Understanding the promotion center | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Promotions & Deals › Understanding the promotion center Understanding the promotion center The Promotions Center on the noon Partner Platform centralizes promotional campaigns and sales performance tracking. Sellers can view real-time performance metrics—such as promo units sold and promo sales generated—while enrolling in noon-managed deals or launching customized, self-serve promotions. Accessing your Promotion Center - Log in to your Seller Lab and in the navigation menu click on Promotions , then select either Promotions Center, My Campaigns, or Upload/Downloads from the dropdown options to navigate to whichever section you want to visit. Understanding the Promotion Center The Promotion Center shows how many SKUs are enrolled and how your campaigns are performing. You can also choose to join an existing deal or build your own deals from the page as well. Key features on Promotion Center: Your Campaign Summary : Monitor units sold on deal items, sales generated from deal items, and total active campaigns over a 7-day or 30-day period. Join an Existing Deal noon Deals : Enroll products in high-traffic category deals to earn deal badges on listings. Flash Deals : Join short-term, high-urgency promotions featuring deeper price discounts and prominent placement. Cofunding Deals : Unlock reduced commission tiers on eligible SKUs funded directly by noon. Other Campaigns : Browse additional promotional formats such as promo codes or clickable coupons campaigns that run on the platform. Build Your Own Discounts Vouchers : Set up discount vouchers displayed directly on product pages. Price Drop Discount : Apply custom price drops to selected tags to drive sales volume. Bundle Discount : Group complementary products at a special price to increase your sales. Key features on My Campaigns: Legacy Campaigns: You will be able to view and manage the campaigns in which you enrolled using the previous tool. Campaigns and Products: You can also check the campaigns and the products which will you have enrolled using the new tool. Key features on Uploads / Downloads : Upload : You can upload the deals in bulk using the upload option. Download : You can request an export from the specific deals page, then return to this section to download your Excel file once it's ready. FAQs Q. What is the new Promotion Center? A- The Promotion Center provides a clear, centralized view of your active campaigns and performance metrics. Enrolling in noon deals is simple: select a week, choose your eligible items, or set a custom duration. Q. When is this going to be live? A- We’ve rolled this out to all sellers in the UAE, KSA, and Egypt in the first week of October. Q. How do discount rules and deal eligibility work? A- For noon deals, you will see a recommended target price required for eligibility. You can submit an item into a deal once its price is set at or below that recommended level. Q. What is Price Drop and how does it work? A- Price Drop is an instant discount tool where you set your own price for an item, and it goes live immediately with no approval needed from noon. This is ideal when you want to run discounts on specific catalog items that don't meet full deal pricing criteria. Q. What happened to my existing campaigns and enrollments? A- All active promotions, deals, and coupons carried over automatically during the update. Nothing was paused, reset, or required re-enrollment. Q. Can I still access my historical performance data? A- Yes. All past campaign and performance data remains fully intact and accessible in your dashboard. Q. Who do I contact if I need support? A- Reach out to your account manager or noon Seller Support for assistance with campaign setup or navigation. That’s it! Got questions? Contact us at seller@noon.com
