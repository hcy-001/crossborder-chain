Article to understand the eligibility and the seller profile to be able to sell on noon noon is open to businesses and individuals across the UAE, KSA, and Egypt. Before you begin registration, use this guide to confirm you're eligible and understand what category you fall under. Categories of Sellers You can register as a seller on noon under one of three categories: Local Seller — Your business is registered in the country where you want to sell (UAE, KSA, or Egypt). International Seller — Your business is registered outside the country where you want to sell. Individual Seller — Available in Egypt only, using your personal Data Certificate. Eligibility Checklist Before you register, confirm that you meet the following requirements: 1. Commercial Registration You must provide a valid registration document based on your seller category: Local Sellers: Must have a valid Commercial Registration (CR) or Trade License issued in the country where you intend to sell. Your license must include at least one of the following activities: Sales, Trading, Manufacturing, or Distribution/Wholesale. International Sellers: Must provide a valid Trade License or Commercial Certificate from your business's country of registration. Individual Sellers (Egypt only): Must provide a valid personal Data Certificate instead of a business license. Note: All registration documents must have at least 15 days of remaining validity at the time of submission. 2. Tax Compliance VAT-registered sellers: You'll need your Tax Registration Number (TRN), along with the issue and expiry dates of your VAT Certificate. Non-VAT sellers: You can proceed by selecting the “Non-VAT” option during registration. Note that Egyptian sellers must always provide their Tax Card information. 3. Identity Verification A Business Representative must be assigned to manage your account. Accepted ID documents: Passport, Saudi National ID, or Emirates ID. If your representative is not the legal owner named on your Trade License, you must upload a Power of Attorney (POA) authorizing them to act on your behalf. 4. Payment Setup You need a verified payment method to receive payouts from your sales. Accepted documents: A cancelled cheque, bank statement, or bank letterhead. The beneficiary name on your bank document must match the company name on your Trade License. Not Sure If You're Eligible? If you've been flagged as ineligible or want to check a specific requirement, see Ineligibility Issues and How to Resolve Them . Ready to Proceed? Once you've confirmed your eligibility, your next step is to gather your documents and begin registration.
