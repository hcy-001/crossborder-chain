Product Rating is the average of all ratings for a specific product regardless of who sold the item. Product rating reflects on the product, not on the sellers’ performance. This rating will show the public’s opinion on the products. This metric will allow you to see which products are performing well in the marketplace and help you optimize your product range. Customers can also submit written feedback related to their experience with the product. These reviews will be publicly available and visible to both customers and sellers. How is the rating calculated? Product rating is not used to measure your seller performance. An average of all product ratings in your catalog will be used to calculate your Catalog Rating. All product reviews must follow our product guidelines. If you notice any reviews including abusive language or promotional content on your feedback page, contact us at seller@noon.com and we’ll get right back to you.
