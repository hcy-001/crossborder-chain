The FBN Inventory Dashboard offers sellers complete visibility of their saleable and non-saleable inventory stored in Noon warehouses. This guide explains how to access and use the dashboard to effectively track and manage your inventory. Accessing the FBN Inventory Dashboard - In Seller Lab, click the three lines on the top left corner of the screen, select Fulfilled by Noon and then click My Inventory. The dashboard provides a summary of saleable and non-saleable inventory. You can: Filter inventory by warehouse. Search for products using PSKU (partner SKU), SKU (ZSKU) , or Pbarcode. Dashboard Features and Information Overall saleable and non-saleable summary: This dashboard gives a complete overview of the inventory available in the saleable or non-saleable stock. Saleable Inventory The Saleable Tab displays information about SKUs that passed the quality check upon receiving items in the noon warehouse and are ready to sell. Key details include: Dimensions : Volumetric measurements of the item (longest, median, shortest sides) and size classification. FBN and VMS Fees : Estimated fees charged by Noon (viewable under the "View Fees" option). Inventory Type : Specifies whether the stock is saleable or non-saleable. Warehouse : Identifies where the inventory is stored. Saleable Quantity : Number of units ready for sale. Total Stock : Overall stock for a particular SKU in the selected warehouse. Non-Saleable Inventory The Non-Saleable Tab shows items that failed the quality check, either at inbound or due to customer returns. Categories of Non-Saleable Inventory All : Full list of non-saleable items. Stranded Issue : Items stored as non-saleable for over 30 days will incur storage fees. Content Issue : Items that differ from their catalog listing.
