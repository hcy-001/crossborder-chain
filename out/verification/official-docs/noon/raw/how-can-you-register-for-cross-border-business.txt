The primary goal for any business is growth, aiming to secure a significant market share. Engaging in cross-border business facilitates selling your products in different countries. This article will primarily guide you on how to register and establish your cross-border business with noon Note: The cross-border business is restricted only to UAE and KSA as of now. In order to start selling as a cross-border seller you must submit your Tax Details of the country you wish to sell in. You can do so by following these simple steps. Step 1: Create a New Store in the cross-border country you wish to sell in Step 2 : Add VAT certificate of the country you wish to sell to the existing legal entity(LE) and submit for approval Step 3 : Link Legal Entity to Store Step 4 : Complete T&C Process Steps to register for a cross-border business Step1: Create a New Store in the cross-border country you wish to sell in - Click on the Country selector on the seller lab and, then click on Add New Store and select the country where you want to open the store. - Create your noon store by filling in all the details requested. Click on Continue to proceed. Step2: Add VAT certificate of the country you wish to sell to the existing legal entity(LE) and submit for approval: - Click on Store Management section and select the Legal option. - In the Legal section, click on the Legal Entities option. Then click on the Action button - In the Legal Entity page, click on Tax Details section and click on Add New. - Make sure to select the correct Tax country and choose VAT registered in the VAT type. Then click on Save Changes. Note: For cross border selling the VAT details are mandatory. Non-VAT registered sellers cannot do the cross border business. This does not apply in UAE to KSA lane. - Once the Tax details and updated you can click on Submit for Approval . The approval process will be completed within 24- 48 hours. Step3: Link Legal Entity to Store: You will receive an email confirmation once the Legal Entity is approved. Next you need to ‘ Link ’ your Legal Entity to your store. You can do so by following these simple steps. - Click on the Store Management section and then select the Legal option as shown in the image below. - You can click on the Legal Entities section and then click on Unlink LE . Then click on Proceed . Step4: Complete T&C Process: - If the Legal Entity is already approved the T&Cs are automatically triggered to the Business Representative. - As a last step, you will need to accept the T&Cs which you received on your registered email ID. You will be successfully onboarded and can now start listing your products. That’s it! Got questions? Reach out to seller@noon.com
