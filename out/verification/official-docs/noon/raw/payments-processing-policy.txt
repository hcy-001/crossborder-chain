Accessing Statements You can access your statements via Seller Lab under the ‘Statements ’ tab in the Payment and Fees section. Each statement has a unique reference number reflecting its issuance date. Payments to Sellers noon processes payments weekly for sellers who meet the following conditions: Payment Conditions Positive Weekly Statement : Your sales revenue (after returns) must exceed the fees due. Minimum Payment Threshold : To receive your weekly payout, your accumulated earnings must meet a minimum threshold based on your bank account currency. Payout Thresholds by Account Type Bank Account Currency Marketplace Currency Minimum Threshold Local Currency (Your bank account currency is the same as your marketplace currency) UAE (AED) KSA (SAR) Egypt (EGP) 100 AED / SAR / EGP International Currency (Your bank account currency is different from your marketplace currency) UAE (AED) KSA (SAR) 1,000 AED / SAR Note: Payments will appear in your bank account within 1 to 2 business days from the due date. Please note that public or bank holidays may extend this processing time. Example: Local vs. International Payouts Let’s look at how the threshold applies to two different sellers: Seller A (Local Account): Sells on the KSA marketplace and registers a bank account in SAR. - Result: Their minimum payout threshold is 100 SAR. If they make 100 SAR this week, their payment is processed automatically on the due date. Seller B (International Account): Sells on the UAE marketplace while registering a foreign-currency bank account for payouts - Result : Because the bank is in a different currency than the marketplace, this is classified as an International Payment. Their minimum payout threshold is 1,000 AED//SAR. - Payout Logic: If Seller B makes 400 AED in week 1, the funds are held. If they make another 700 AED in week 2 (bringing the total to 1,100 AED), the threshold is met, and the full 1,100 AED will be released in the next weekly payout cycle. Important Payout Schedule Note: Note: This applies once the statement is generated on Wednesday and the minimum thresholds are met. UAE & KSA: Payouts are processed on Thursday of the following week. Egypt (EGY): Payouts are processed on Tuesday of the following week. What happens if my balance is below the threshold? If your earnings for the week do not meet the minimum amount required for your account type, your funds will be safely held in your account balance. They will automatically roll over to the next billing cycle and will be released once the cumulative total meets or exceeds the threshold.
