This guide walks you through the steps of creating and managing Advance Shipment Notes (ASN), scheduling transfers, and utilizing storage efficiently on Noon’s Seller Lab. It also explains common errors to avoid and the statuses your ASN might go through. Storage Classification: noon categorizes storage by size and item type: Item type classification Food and Beverages – Food, drinks, and other consumable products. HRV (High Retail Value) – Mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and other high-value electronic devices. Over the Counter (OTC) – Items that require cold storage. Installation – Products that require professional installation, assembly, or setup. Over-the-counter – Items that can be sold directly to customers without a prescription. Apparel and Footwear (Only in AE) – Products in the “Apparel” and “Footwear” categories, available only in AE. Not allowed – Products that are prohibited or not eligible for sale or delivery. Default – Products that do not fall under any of the categories above. Item size classification: Products are classified into one of the three size-based classifications based on the dimensions and weight of the product. Maximum Dimension and weight threshold for size classification Size classification (Storage type) Length (cm) Width (cm) Height (cm) Max shipping weight (kg) Standard 45 34 26 12 Oversize 130 60 50 30 Bulky >130 >60 >50 >30 Length, Width and height are the longest, median, and shortest side, respectively. Accessing the ASN & Storage Dashboard - In the Navigation Menu, click on the Fulfilled by Noon (FBN) tab and select My ASN & Storage . You will see five tabs: Shipments : Create, edit, and schedule ASNs or track pending ones. Storage : View available, utilized, and in-transit storage. Requests : Manage and track extra storage requests. Product Dimensions : View and update product dimension details. Serialization Service: Set the serial number which will be used in the inbound process. Below are details of each tab: 1. Shipments a. Open ASN's that have been created but not yet sealed, scheduled, or still pending (an extra storage request is pending for this ASN) b. View all scheduled ASN's and have the option to cancel or review them c. Search for a specific ASN, sort ASNs by creation date (newest to oldest or oldest to newest), or filter ASNs by status. 2. Storage This tab displays details of all storage types, including available, utilized, and in-transit space. 3. Request Allows users to view any extra storage requests they have made, and search, filter, or sort these requests as needed. 4. This will be helpful to support the serialization service during inbound in the noon warehouse How to Create an ASN In the Navigation Menu, click on Fulfilled by noon, then select Create ASN . Option A: Select Items from Catalog Choose " Select in the UI ." Manually select products, enter quantities, and update any missing volume details. Then click on Continue . The ASN will be created, and you will have the option to view the details of the ASN. Click on it and in the details page you will get an option to update the quantity as well. Click on Save changes. Click on Continue anyways and then click on Create ASN. A pop-up appears and you need to click on the Create ASN button. The ASN is created, and you can click on Details to see the ASN information. You also have an option to edit , cancel , or seal it . If you choose to seal the ASN, you will need to schedule it. Click on Seal to proceed. This will seal the ASN Then click the View ASN button, and the ASN status will be updated to Sealed. Note: When submitting an ASN, the quantity of products included must not exceed the maximum quantity specified in the product's max quantity field. Requesting Extra Storage If your items exceed your current storage space, click Request Extra Storage during ASN creation. Choose to either: Remove items from the ASN. Submit an extra storage request for Noon’s approval. Once approved, seal and proceed with scheduling the ASN. For any rejected request, edit your ASN to match the available storage. Scheduling the ASN 1. After sealing the ASN, click Schedule on the ASN details page. 2. Select a warehouse with available space and choose a date and time slot. Click Confirm Slot to finalize the schedule. Warehouse addresses/locations for ASN drop-offs. Note: The available warehouses (WHs) will be displayed in Seller Lab . Please make sure to book your ASN in one of the warehouses listed there. Availability depends on storage space and capacity. 3. The ASN will be successfully scheduled and you will be taken back to the ASN details page with the scheduled date and time displayed. Rescheduling Your ASN If you miss your delivery slot: Go to the ASN details page and click Reschedule . A pop-up appears to confirm if you want to reschedule the ASN. Click on Yes , Reschedule to continue. Select a warehouse, new date and time slot, and click confirm slot . Your ASN is now rescheduled to the new slot and the original slot will become available for other sellers. Printing Transfer Documentation Once the ASN is scheduled you should print the transfer documentation and prepare your products for dispatch to noon with your chosen logistics partner or your own transportation means. You have 2 ways to print the ASN: From the ASN page: click Print ASN to print required documents. From the list of ASNs: go to All ASNs under the ASN & storage dashboard , you will see the status of the ASN which are scheduled, click on the action button and select Print option to print the document needed for the ASN transfer . Troubleshooting : If the print option doesn’t appear, refresh the page or try a different browser. ASN Statuses In the process flow the ASN status will be changing depending on the stage of the process. The status you will fine are: ASN Status Explanation Created The ASN is newly created, or an already existing ASN which is not yet sealed will show this status. Sealed Once you have made the selection of the SKUs and its quantity to be transferred, you need to seal the ASN. Once the ASN is sealed, it can no longer be edited. But it can still be cancelled or scheduled for delivery. Scheduled This status means that the ASN is scheduled to be delivered to the respective noon fulfillment center on the selected slot. Pending This status is found in case the ASN is having some pending request for additional space attached to the ASN. Handed Over This status indicates that warehouse agents have unloaded and gated the ASN products from the seller's vehicle. Receiving ASN products are going through a quality control check and being received in the warehouse. Putaway in Progress ASN products are being put away in their designated inventory locations. GRN Completed The receiving and putaway process is completed for the ASN with inventory being available on the marketplace. At this stage, seller will receive the GRN report too. Cancelled The seller has an option to cancel the ASN at any point of the process flow. However, if the ASN is close to the scheduled date or is already expired the ASN cannot be cancelled. Expired You may find this status in 2 scenarios 1. A ‘Sealed’ ASN will expire in 60 days if not scheduled, counting from the time it was sealed. 2. A ‘Scheduled’ ASN will expire 2 days after the scheduled date (the date selected by the seller to ship their products to the warehouse). FAQ Q: Can I reallocate my unused storage space from one inventory type to another, such as from bulky to standard? A: No, reallocating unused storage space between different inventory types is not allowed. noon categorizes storage separately by item size and type to maintain operational efficiency — for example, standard, oversize, and bulky storage each have their own allocations. If you require additional space for a specific inventory type, please create an ASN (Advanced Shipment Notice) through Seller Lab, add the necessary items, and, if your storage exceeds the available limit, request extra storage during the ASN creation process. noon will review and approve or reject extra storage requests accordingly. Q : What is the address of the warehouse where I can send my FBN goods/products? A: If you're using Fulfilled by Noon (FBN) and have created an ASN (Advance Shipping Note) for your items, you can select a warehouse with available space during the ASN scheduling process.
