Once an item... The Sales View allows you to keep track of all of your sales and payments through noon. Once an item is dispatched to the customer, it will be visible in this view. This page includes sales under all selling models, whether Fulfilled by noon, Fulfilled by Partner, or Supermall. The sales view will allow you to do the following View consolidated sales for a particular product. Check item statuses in real-time and the important time stamps. Accessing the Sales View You can access the Sales view by following these simple steps. - Click on Account Health and Report and then click on Sales. - You have an option to select the store for which you can see the sales. - You will have filters available, which help you to refine your search results on this page Filters Explanation Search You can search the SKU to check the number of sales for that SKU. Date range You can select a date range to view the sales within that range. Fulfillment Selecting the fulfillment model, which can be either Fulfillment by noon, Fulfilled by Partner or Supermall Destination The destination where the item is going to be delivered. Category The product category or the family type of the SKU Brand Brand of the SKU Status Status of the sale You can refer to the table below to understand the statuses of each sale. Status Explanation Pending confirmation Order has been placed and confirmation is pending due to payment processing Confirmed Order is confirmed Processing Order under review before moving to fulfillment Shipped Order is shipped Delivered Order is successfully delivered to the customer Could not be delivered Order could not be delivered due to some reason Cancelled Order cancelled by the customer - You can also export the details on the sales page as an Excel file by using the Export option. - We further scroll down, we will have the details of the sale, which includes the SKU details, PSKU number or the item number, Category of the SKU, Price of the SKU, Destination of the sale, Ordered date when the order was placed, Shipping date when the order was shipped, Delivery date which is the date when the order was delivered and the Status of the order. - If you wish to see more details of the specific sale, which include the fees charged or the reference number, you can click on the Transaction View option .
