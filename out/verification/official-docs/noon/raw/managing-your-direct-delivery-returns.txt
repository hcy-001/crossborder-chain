Managing your direct delivery returns | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Fulfilled by Partner (FBP) › Managing your direct delivery returns Managing your direct delivery returns Managing customer return requests for Direct Delivery orders is simple once you know the steps. This guide walks you through every stage of the return process, from receiving a request to selecting the final verdict, so you never miss an SLA or a customer request. Note : This process is live for some selected sellers only currently. How Return Requests Arrive When a customer submits a return request (such as an Exceptional Return Case along with a statement and photo attachments): Email Notification: You receive an automated email notifying you of the new request. Where can you see this: Go to Fulfilled by Partner, click Direct Delivery shipment, and then select the specific Direct Delivery warehouse . Then click on Return Requests Status & SLA: The new request appears in Pending status with an active live Service Level Agreement (SLA) countdown timer. To better understand each status refer to this table. Return Details Page: Click on any Order ID number to view complete details: Return status, order number, and AWB number Customer contact information and delivery address, and statement Return reason Uploaded photos, item details, and full activity timeline Scheduled visit dates and final verdicts (once updated) Managing the pending status Every new return starts in the Pending status. You have 3 days to contact the customer and take action. You will receive daily reminder emails until you act. Important SLA Warning: If you take no action within the 3-day window, the request automatically expires and the customer is auto-refunded . Available Actions in Pending State: Schedule Visit: When to use: Use this when you have contacted the customer and agreed on a pickup/visit date. How to submit: Click on Schedule Visit; a pop-up appears where you can select a date within the next 5 days and choose one of the available time slots. Click Confirm Date . The status changes to Scheduled 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM 7:00 PM – 10:00 PM Note : Scheduled dates cannot be rescheduled once confirmed, so always double-check with the customer first. Mark Non-Responsive: When to use: Use this if you cannot reach the customer after 3 separate contact attempts . How to submit: Click the dropdown menu next to Schedule Visit , select Non-responsive , and click Confirm . What happens next: This pauses your SLA countdown and escalates the request to Seller Support . Managing the scheduled actions & final verdicts Once a visit is scheduled, you must submit a final verdict within 3 days after the visit date . Daily reminder emails will be sent until a verdict is submitted. Important SLA Warning: If you fail to select a verdict before the deadline closes, the request status moves to Customer Refund in Progress , actions are disabled, and the customer is auto-refunded . Available Verdict Actions: You can check the available verdicts once you schedule the visit. Post scheduling you will get a drop-down which shows the available verdict you can take. Mark as returned You can click on Mark Returned on the actions tab. This will open up a pop-up showing a disclaimer that - marking this shipment as returned would issue an instant refund to the customer. Click on Confirm to proceed with refunding the customer. Item repaired: If the item is repaired, you can click on Item repaired and then upload Proof of delivery of the repaired item, then click on Confirm . Item replaced: If the item is replaced, you can click on Item replaced and then upload Proof of delivery of the replaced item, then click on Confirm. Non-responsive: If the customer is non-responsive, you can select this option and the same will be updated in the return details for the seller support to take the necessary action. Proof of 3 contact attempts. Escalated to Seller Support (SLA paused). Escalation flow: Non-responsive customers When you mark a customer as non-responsive: The SLA countdown pauses. A non-responsive icon displays next to the customer's name, and the action button changes to View Details . Clicking on view details button will open the return details and the banner on the top shows that the customer is non-responsive. Seller Support is notified and will reach out to the customer on your behalf. Seller Support will resolve the ticket in one of two ways: Reset to Pending: Moves the return back to Pending status (your 3-day SLA countdown resumes). Cancel Request: Cancels the return request.
