Downloading the global catalog export- Indian sellers | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Global Selling › Downloading the global catalog export- Indian sellers Downloading the global catalog export- Indian sellers To download your SKU list from the Seller Lab via GlobalShip, follow these organized steps. This process is essential for keeping your stock and pricing data synchronized. Step-by-Step Guide Access Seller Lab: Log in to your seller account with your credentials. Navigate to Global Catalog : - Click on Catalog and then click on My Catalog. - Go to the Global Tab within the dashboard. Initiate Export : - Look for the Export button located on the left-hand side of the screen and click it. - Click on New Export in the pop-up that appears Download : Once the system processes the request, a file containing all your PSKUs (noon SKUs) will be available for download. Click on download to export the file. That’s it! Got more questions? Contact us at seller@noon.com
