Once you start making sales on noon, we will process your payment in two ways depending on the value of your weekly statement. To receive your weekly payout, your accumulated earnings must meet a minimum threshold based on your bank account currency. Payout Thresholds by Account Type Bank Account Currency Marketplace Currency Minimum Threshold Local Currency (Your bank account currency is the same as your marketplace currency) UAE (AED) KSA (SAR) Egypt (EGP) 100 AED / SAR / EGP International Currency (Your bank account currency is different from your marketplace currency) UAE (AED) KSA (SAR) 1,000 AED / SAR Note: Payments will appear in your bank account within 1 to 2 business days from the due date. Please note that public or bank holidays may extend this processing time. For more details on the payment processing, please refer to our article on the same. Payment statements are issued weekly and are accessible on your Seller Lab, under Payment and Fees > Statements . Each statement has its reference number that reflects the date of its issuance. Listed below are the steps to help you access your all your payments: - On Payment and Fees, then select Statements. - You will be redirected to the statements page, where you can view all the statements that are generated and the payment that was transferred to your account.
