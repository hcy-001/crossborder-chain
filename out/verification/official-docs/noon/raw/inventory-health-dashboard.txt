The Inventory Health Dashboard is a powerful tool designed to help sellers resolve inventory-related issues by providing recommendations for pricing improvements and stock updates. Whether you're selling through Fulfilled by Noon (FBN) or Fulfilled by Partner (FBP) models, the dashboard delivers real-time suggestions to optimize your inventory management. Key Features of the Inventory Health Dashboard Recommendations for pricing improvements and stock replenishments. Daily updates to ensure real-time visibility. Categorized views for streamlined navigation: Pricing Improvement FBP Replenishment FBN Replenishment Important Notes: Once you take action on a SKU, it will no longer appear on the dashboard. However, it may reappear if noon updates the recommendations. Initially, only selected SKUs with suitable recommendations are displayed. More SKUs will be added as the functionality expands. Accessing the Inventory Health Dashboard To access the dashboard, follow these steps: Go to the Account Health and Report tab in the navigation toolbar. Click on the Inventory Health tab. Views in the Inventory Health Dashboard The dashboard is divided into three main views, each tailored to specific aspects of inventory management: 1. Pricing Improvement 2. FBP Replenishment 3. FBN Replenishment I. Pricing Improvement This view highlights SKUs requiring price updates for better performance in the marketplace. SKUs in this view are tagged with one of the following reasons: Non-Buy Box (BB) Not Priced Competitively (Comp) : Missing the Buy Box (BB): Not Priced Competitively (Comp) : This Schedule will show the following information for each SKU in the list. Set Price Manually : Use the Set Price option in the drop-down menu. Enter the updated price in the price box (a recommended price will be displayed). Click Update Pricing to save changes. Pricing Engine : Select the Pricing Engine option from the drop-down menu. Provide a Min-Max price range for the SKU. A recommended range will be displayed. Ensure your price range is within the recommended limits and click Update Pricing . Note: Accept the Pricing Engine Terms & Conditions in Seller Lab before using this feature. FBP Replenishment This view is exclusive for the SKUs you are selling under the FBP selling model. We identify the SKUs that need replenishment and we classify them based on their urgency. The level of urgency is as follows: Out of Stock: These SKUs are offline on the platform due to a lack of stock. Risk of Out of Stock: These SKUs are fast-selling and can be out of Stock soon. Thus, a replenishment for the stock is recommended. Normal Replenishment: The SKUs under this urgency level can be replenished at a regular pace to avoid the risk of the SKU going out of stock in the future. You can use the View-Specific filter, which is based on these levels of urgency, to filter out the SKUs that need immediate attention and replenishment. How to update the stock under FBP Replenishment? You can easily update the stock for these FBP SKUs by following these simple steps: - You will find a recommended quantity for each SKU, suggested by noon to be replenished, which is based on the historical sales of the item. - You may choose to update the recommended quantity or even increase it, depending on your feasibility. You can click on the Selected Only option to view all the SKUs you selected to update the stock, then click on the Update Stock button. III. FBN Replenishment This view lists SKUs stored in Noon’s warehouse that require stock updates via ASN transfers. Note: SKUs without a Partner Barcode or volumetric measurements, and bulky items, are excluded from this list Urgency Levels for Replenishment Out of Stock : SKUs are offline due to a lack of stock. Risk of Out of Stock : Fast-selling SKUs are likely to go out of stock soon. Normal Replenishment : SKUs requiring regular replenishment to avoid the risk of the SKU going out of stock in the future. You can use the View-Specific filter, which is based on these levels of urgency, to filter out the SKUs that need immediate attention and replenishment. How to update the stock under FBN replenishment? How to Update Stock Under FBN Replenishment Review the recommended quantity for each SKU based on historical sales. You may choose to update the recommended quantity or even increase it Use the Selected Only option to view selected SKUs. Click Create ASN to initiate an ASN transfer. Important Notes: If your ASN exceeds available storage capacity, request extra storage by clicking Confirm. SKUs tagged as Not Scheduled will remain in this status until the ASN is sealed and scheduled. To schedule pending ASNs: Click on the Schedule Now hyperlink in the notification bar. Seal and schedule the ASN for transfer to the respective Noon warehouse. You can refer to this article to understand and schedule the ASN on the Seller Lab, General Filters and Sorting In addition to view-specific filters, the dashboard provides general filtering and sorting options for all three views. How to Use Filters - Click the Filter button on the right side of the screen. - A pop-up will appear with multiple filters, such as Family , Brand , Visibility , and Marketplace SKU Priority . Select your desired filters and click Apply. How to Use Sorting? Click the Sort button next to the Filter option. Select your preferred sorting criteria from the drop-down menu.
