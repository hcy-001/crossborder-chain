How to add the payout method in the legal entity? Ensuring smooth payment transactions is essential for maintaining a healthy business on Noon. A key ... When you sell on noon, you receive the payments based on where your products ship from—known as your selling origin . If your legal entity sells from multiple origins, each origin needs an approved payout method to receive payments. Until a payout method is set up and approved for an origin, your sales will accumulate, but noon will not be able to transfer your earnings. Here is a simple, step-by-step guide to adding and linking a payout method to your seller origin in Seller Lab. Step 1: Add a Payout Method Log in to Seller Lab. Go to Store Management , then click Legal . Select the legal entity you are currently selling with. Click the Payout Methods tab. Click Add payout method (if you already have a payout method on file, click Add New in the top right corner). Select your preferred payout method from the list. Fill in the required account details, and upload the necessary documents. The click on Save changes. Click Submit for Approval. Supported Payout Methods noon supports the following payout options: Bank Account Payoneer LianLian WorldFirst PingPong Note: Make sure your uploaded documents match noon's accepted document requirements. Your payout method must be reviewed and approved by noon before it can be used. Step 2: Selecting your payout method for your selling origin Adding and approving a payout method isn't the final step—you must assign it to your specific seller origin to start receiving funds. In the Payout Methods tab, locate your approved payout method. Go to the Receiving Payments column. Click the origin dropdown menu to view all the origins associated with your legal entity. Select the origin(s) you want this specific payout method to receive payments for. Once your payout method is approved and mapped to your origin, your setup is complete, and you are ready to receive payouts! That’s it! Got questions? Reach out to seller@noon.com .
