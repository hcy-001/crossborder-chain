Warranty Fees (WNTY) A charge for warranty claims made by the customers which the seller couldn't fulfill as per the local law and noon's policies 4. Technician Inspection Failure Fee Note : This fee will be effective from 1st November 2026 . To maintain high-quality standards for Renewed & Refurbished products sold via Fulfilled by noon (FBN), inbound shipments undergo a technical inspection upon arrival at the fulfillment center. For this reason, FBN sellers will incur a Technician Inspection Failure Fee only when a unit is rejected during inspection due to issues like functional defects, while inventory that passes the inspection will incur no charge. UAE rate: 1% of the ASP per unit of the last 30 days or 13 AED, whichever is higher. 5. Retention Penalty fees (RETP) The Retention Penalty Fee (RETP) is charged to the seller to compensate our customers for grievances arising from non-adherence to local laws and our policies. These grievances can be due to, but are not limited to, items that are missing parts/accessories, counterfeit, damaged, defective, used, expired, not matching the product listing, or having pricing errors. 6. Miscellaneous Fees (MISC) An amount charged to the seller for any irregular occurrences that they may cause, which engender grievances to our customer, or/ and against the local laws and noon policies , such as counterfeit penalties.
