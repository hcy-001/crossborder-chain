noon deals: how to enroll your products | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Promotions & Deals › noon deals: how to enroll your products noon deals: how to enroll your products noon deals are campaigns run by noon to boost visibility for enrolled products. Your products appear on noon's deals page with a deal badge for the week you choose. A deeper discount unlocks stronger placement. Why join a noon deal Your product is listed on noon's high-traffic category deals page for the full deal period. The product carries a deal badge on its listing, which shoppers see in search and on the product page. You choose the schedule and the products. noon sets the price ceiling and reviews what you commit. Two visibility tiers: Standard visibility and Highest visibility . The deeper the discount, the higher the tier. You can enroll products one by one in Seller Lab or in bulk with an Excel file. How enrollment works Enrollment has two steps: pick the schedule , then add your products . Nothing is saved until you click Submit campaign . Step 1: Select schedule In the Promotions Center, find noon deals under Join an existing deal and click Join deal . Choose one of the two tabs. Select duration, which contains a list of upcoming deal weeks set by noon, each with its name (for example, Everyday Deals, Big Deal Sale, Singles Day) and the number of products you already have enrolled in that week. Custom duration, which allows you to pick your own start and end date under Your own dates, or join one of the custom-duration deals already listed. Select the week or dates you want. The bar at the bottom confirms your choice under Selected. Click Select products. Step 2: Add products The header shows the deal type, the deal week, the deal code (for example, nd-ae-20261003-20261010), and a link to view the products you already have enrolled. Find the products you want to enroll. Search by partner SKU, or tick the checkbox at the top of the list to select the whole page. For each product, enter the Deal price . The maximum allowed is shown under the field as Max . The price cannot be higher than the Max. Read the Deal tier column. One flame means Standard visibility and two flames mean Highest visibility. The percentage under the flames is the discount against the base price. Discount per unit is calculated for you as base price minus deal price. Enter the Committed units for each product. Your current stock is shown under the field. Click Submit campaign . Enroll in bulk with Excel On the Add products page, click Export this list . The export contains every eligible product for the deal week with its base price and maximum deal price. Open the file, fill out the deal price and deal stock for the products you want in the exported sheet, and save it. Click Upload , and in the pop-up that appears choose your file, and click Upload . You can also upload from Promotions > Uploads/downloads . Click on Upload/ downloads to check the upload status. Rows with errors show the reason next to them; fix the row and upload again. Example Product Portable power bank Base price AED 189 Deal week Sat 3 Oct to 10 Oct Maximum deal price (Max) AED 95.04 Deal price you enter AED 92.07 Discount 51%, which places the product in the Highest visibility tier Discount per unit AED 96.93 Committed units 50 During the deal week The product shows the deal badge and sits on the category deals page at AED 92.07 After the deal Once the 50 committed units sell, or the week ends, the product returns to its normal price What happens after you submit noon reviews the prices you commit. Accepted products go live on the deal start date and carry the badge until the deal ends. You can follow the status of each product in the campaign under My campaigns , and then click on Manage. To make any change to a price or committed units, you can click on the edit option for the respective SKUs to make the changes. Why are some products not shown? The Add products list shows only products that are eligible for the selected deal week. A product is left out when: it is not live on noon; for example, it is out of stock or failed a catalog check. it has no partner SKU price range, so noon cannot set a maximum deal price.
