How to create noon product listings using web URLs? Tired of copying and pasting product details line-by-line? With our new Web URL Product Creation tool, you can automatically convert any of your existing product webpages into a fully structured noon listing. The AI extracts your product’s images, title, description, and maps them directly to noon’s required attributes. How to Create a Product from a Web URL? You can create a product listing with the web URL option by following these simple steps: - Navigate to the Tool: Log in to noon Partners, and in the navigation menu, select Catalog , then click Add Product Then select the Web URL tab on the Create Products landing page. - Paste Your URL: Copy and paste the link to your existing product page. You should use your own official brand website URL for the best results. Ensure the link you are providing is the ultimate PDP, which is visible to the final customer, for better results. - Accept Terms & Submit: Click on the hyperlink to read the listing terms. Tick the box confirming that you have the right to use the URL’s content and agree to the Listing Terms. Click Submit . - Review & Edit Listing: The AI will analyze the webpage, extract the media and details, and map everything to noon’s catalog attributes. Click Review & Edit Listing once generation completes. Always inspect, adjust, and approve the pre-filled fields, text, and images before saving. Note: All listings created via Web URL extraction still undergo noon’s standard Quality Control (QC) review before going live. What You Need to Know (Best Practices & Limitations) Always Review Before Submitting: AI-generated content is a draft suggestion. The AI may occasionally miss details or interpret text differently than intended. You are responsible for ensuring all product information is accurate, truthful, and compliant with noon's QC standards and local laws. Licensing & Rights: By submitting a URL, you confirm that you own or have explicit permission to use, display, and publish all associated intellectual property (text, images, branding) on noon.
