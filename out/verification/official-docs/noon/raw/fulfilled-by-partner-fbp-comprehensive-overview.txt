In this article, we are going to explore the Fulfilled By Partner (FBP) model on the noon platform. The FBP model is an order fulfillment approach where the inventory is kept at the Partner or Seller's location. In this model, the Partner or Seller is responsible for packaging the order, while noon assists with the logistics needed to deliver the order to the customer, with the exception of Direct Delivery scenarios.
