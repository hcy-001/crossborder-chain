SKU Content Policy We have two types of processes to list your products: 1. noon SKU (NSKU): This is applicable for all noon-controlled branded products where the content is created by you and managed and modified by noon. You can’t modify or delete these SKUs once created. The ownership of content lies with noon and noon reserves the right to modify or delete such SKUs. Such SKUs are provided with unique identification numbers starting with “N” and are referred to as NSKUs. You can copy or map against any existing NSKU where you can just map your SKU against any existing NSKU and continue selling. 2. ZSKU: This is for all kinds of products other than noon-controlled products. You need to create the content of such products by yourself and noon will approve the content post scrutiny. You will be in full control of the content of these kinds of SKUs and can list and modify the content at your discretion. Any product listed under ZSKU will be given a unique identification number starting with “Z” and referred to as ZSKUs. You need to refer to this unique number during any interaction related to this product with noon. Monthly Product Creation Limits To ensure a high-quality marketplace and help sellers build a sustainable business foundation, there is a monthly limit on new product creations. The standard monthly limit is 25,000 new products per month. This approach helps ensure a fair and balanced marketplace, allowing all sellers to establish their products, generate sales, and build a strong reputation before expanding their catalog further. How Variations Count Toward Your Quota Not all product variations are treated equally under the monthly quota. Refer to the table below to see how your listings are counted: Product Setup How It Counts Example / Rule Size Variants (Under a Parent SKU) Counts as 1 product A single Parent SKU with multiple sizes linked underneath = 1 product Color Variants Each color counts individually A shirt available in Red, Blue, and Green = 3 products Independent SKUs (Unlinked) Each SKU counts individually If you list 3 independent SKUs for the exact same item in different sizes (not linked to a Parent SKU), they count as 3 products . Only approved (whitelisted) sellers are allowed to sell these gated brands and categories . If you wish to sell gated products, you can apply for whitelisting . However, noon reserves the right to approve or reject your application. As part of the application process, you may be required to submit one or more of the following documents: Seller Undertaking VAT Certificate Distribution Authorization Agreement Proof of Purchase / Signed and Stamped Invoice Import License Government Regulatory Body Certificate Manufacturer Permit / Certificate Note: The decision made by noon regarding whitelisting is final and binding. Q: What Types of Gating Exist on noon? A: There are two types of gating on noon: 1. Brand Gating Brand Gating limits the sale of products from specific brands to protect against counterfeit goods , unauthorized sellers, and to maintain brand integrity . Only authorized or verified sellers can list products from gated brands. 2. Category Gating Category Gating restricts the sale of products in certain categories that are sensitive , regulated , or have stricter quality control requirements . Sellers must meet specific criteria or obtain approval to sell in these categories. Q: Do jewelry and sunglasses require certificates or testing? A: No, they don’t.
