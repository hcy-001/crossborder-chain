Return to vendor (RTV) report | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Fulfilled by noon (FBN) › Return to vendor (RTV) report Return to vendor (RTV) report When you transfer stock to noon’s warehouses, it remains your property and can be retrieved at any time, subject to retrieval fees. To provide transparency and clarity, Noon offers two reports in Seller Lab to help you manage and track retrieval requests: RTV Request Reports and RTV Shipment Reports . This guide explains how to access and download these reports and includes a detailed breakdown of the information provided in each. How to download the RTV report? You can easily access the RTV reports from the Seller Lab, providing clarity on the stock requested for retrieval. To download these reports, follow these simple steps: - Click on the Navigation menu , then select FBN and choose the Reports. - Click on Generate Report . - Under Report Name , select a category and choose Returns . From the Returns Reports tab, select one of the following: RTV Report - Requests RTV Report - Shipments - Choose a Date Range and then click on Generate Report to complete the process. Kindly ensure you choose a date range ending yesterday or earlier, as pulling a report for the current day will return no data.
