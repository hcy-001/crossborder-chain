Setting your contact preferences | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Onboarding & Registration › Setting your contact preferences Setting your contact preferences Staying updated with the latest e-commerce news and developments, especially those related to your performance, is key to success. Our user-friendly contact preference options allow you to customize contact details, including email and phone numbers, across different operational domains, ensuring you receive targeted communication specific to your needs. How To Setup Your Contact Preferences? In this section, we will guide you through the step-by-step process of setting up your contact preference for different domains. - In the Seller Lab navigation bar, click on Store Management and then click on Contact Preferences. - On the next page, click on Add Preferences . - A pop-up will appear, where you can select the Domain for which you want to set up your preference. - Then, select the Contact Type ( email or phone number ). Sellers can now add up to two phone numbers for each domain, which is accessible by admin users only. - Enter the email ID or phone number you wish to add as a preference. - Then select the Preferred Language. - Then click on Submit. Note: 1. There's a limit to the number of contacts per domain; exceeding this may cause an error. 2. At least one email ID is required per domain, and duplicating emails for the same domain is not permitted. 3. Adding or deleting any contact will trigger an email notification to the respective email address. 4. Contact preferences are only for receiving domain-specific emails and do not grant access to Seller Lab. - Your preferred contact will be added to the respective domain, and you can choose to Update the details or Delete the contact from the domain. If only one email ID linked to a domain, it is not permitted to delete it. Types Of Emails You Will Receive By Domain Each domain is accompanied by a description outlining the managed activities. You can carefully review this information and then adjust the contact preferences within your organization for that specific domain. How can I update my email address if I have a new one or no longer have access to my old one? You can update or add a new email address by navigating to the Contact Preferences section in your Seller Lab . Once updated, all future communications from noon —based on your selected domains—will be sent to your new/updated email address(es). Just follow these steps: Log in to your Seller Lab account. Click on Store Management. Click on Contact Preference. Under Contact Preference, click the ‘Add Preference’ button. Select the relevant Domain you'd like to update. From the Contact Type dropdown, choose Email. Choose your Preferred Language – English or Arabic. Click the ‘Submit’ button to save the new email. If needed, delete your old email ID to avoid duplicate communications. That’s it Got more questions? Contact us at seller@noon.com .
