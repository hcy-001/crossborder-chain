Circumstantial fees Circumstantial Fees are fees imposed on sellers by noon for specific conditions or events related to inventory and sales. These fees include non-saleable item storage, warranty claims, miscellaneous irregularities, and retention penalties for non-compliance with local laws and policies. 1- Non-Saleable Item storage Fee: This fee applies to non-saleable inventory stored in noon fulfillment centers for an aging bucket of more that 30 days. We will charge the Non-Saleable Item storage fee based on the cubic feet of the item. Categories EGYPT Cost per CBF*(EGP) Non- saleable Stock 12 *Cubic Feet = Length (cm) x Width (cm) x Height (cm)/28317 2- Warranty (WNTY): A charge for warranty claims made by the customers which the seller couldn’t fulfill as per the local laws and noon’s policies. For warranty policy click here and warranty requirement click here 3- Retention Penalty Fee (RETP) A fee that is charged to the seller to compensate our customers in case they caused them any grievances by not adhering to the local laws and noon’s policies. Such grievances can be as a result of, but not limited to: Cancelling/delaying/not delivering orders. Fulfilling orders using items that have missing parts, or that are fake, damaged, defective, used, expired, not matching your listed product on the website. 4- Return Administration Fee: This fee is charged to the seller for processing items returned by the customer for specific seller-controllable reasons. These reasons include items being different from the listing, fake, damaged, defective, used/pre-activated, missing parts, extra items in the order, exceptional return cases, and post-RTC. The fee will be the lesser of 15 EGP or 20% of the referral fee for the returned item. It will take effect on October 1st, 2024. 5- Miscellaneous fees (MISC) An amount charged to the seller for any irregular occurrences that they may cause, which engender grievances to our customers, or/and are against the local laws and noon policies, such as counterfeit penalties. III. Order Cancellation Fee The cancellation fee for each category will match the FBP referral fee for that category. If a category has an ASP tier, the referral fee for the highest ASP range will be applied. Note: -The fees mentioned in this article will apply to both the Directship model and Bulky Directship. -For direct delivery, only the referral fee will apply, along with circumstantial fees, which may vary depending on specific situations.
