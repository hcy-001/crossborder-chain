All sellers listing under the Renewed program must follow these definitions and grading standards to ensure customer trust and platform consistency. What is 'Renewed'? Renewed refers to products that are lightly used AS-IS stocks or open-box and have been professionally tested, cleaned, and certified before being listed for sale. Renewed devices must not be rebuilt or repaired — all major parts such as the screen, body, and battery must remain original OEM components . They should look and function almost like new , with minimal signs of use, and must pass Noon’s grading guideline standards. Battery must not be replaced — only tested and verified to be ≥85%. Renewed listings must include accurate grading, warranty details, and clear product images. Each SKU must mention the warranty period (12 months) in the product detail page. Sellers are responsible for accurate QC documentation before stock movement or ASN creation. What is 'Refurbished'? Refurbished devices are products that have been technically repaired or rebuilt to factory standards using OEM-grade replacement parts . If the screen, body, or battery was defective, it must be replaced with new OEM components, and the unit must be fully retested before sale. These devices are restored to look and perform exactly like new and must come with a 6-month seller warranty. Key Requirements for Refurbished Devices Parameter Requirement Condition 100% like-new appearance Parts OEM-grade new parts replaced where required (screen, body, battery) Battery 100% new OEM-grade battery Functionality Must pass QC tests after rebuild Cosmetic Condition Flawless; no scratches, dents, or discoloration Warranty 6-month seller warranty Packaging Refurbished box with accessories QC Report Mandatory; must show components replaced and test results Refurbishment Process (Seller Flow) Device Inspection – Diagnose issues and assess repair requirements. Replacement – Replace faulty parts with new OEM-grade components. Testing – Perform full QC tests post-repair. Cleaning & Sanitization – Ensure device is spotless and hygienic. Packaging – Pack in a clean white or brown box with all accessories. Warranty Update – Register a 6-month seller warranty for the unit.
