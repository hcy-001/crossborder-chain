A+ content on noon: Enhancing your product pages | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Product Listing › A+ content on noon: Enhancing your product pages A+ content on noon: Enhancing your product pages A+ Content allows you to upgrade your product pages with rich, visual content to provide a better shopping experience. On noon, this currently supports image banners , which appear on the Product Detail Page (PDP) under the Product Features section, immediately following the specifications. Why use A+ Content? Using A+ Content helps you: Showcase your product visually: Go beyond basic imagery to tell a story. Highlight key selling points: Make your product's unique features stand out. Improve customer confidence: A better understanding leads to higher conversion and fewer returns. How to Locate and Add A+ Content Follow these steps to find and upload A+ Content within the Seller Lab : 1. Select Your Product - Log in to Seller Lab . Click on Catalog and then select My Catalog . - Open the specific product you wish to edit. - Navigate to the Content tab. - Scroll down to the Additional Media section. -In the additional media, you can scroll down further and you will find the option to add A+ content. You can click on Add A+ Content to proceed. 2. Add and Arrange Banners - In the next step, you will find an option to add the banners in English and Arabic language. - Click on Select Image to choose the banner that you want to add. Arrange them in your preferred order; they will appear in a top-to-bottom sequence on the PDP. Note: Do not use the same images that are already in your product carousel. 3. Upload and Validation - Upload your high-quality banner images. Each image is validated automatically upon upload. Make sure the image dimension and aspect ratio is as per the noon standards. If there is an issue (e.g., resolution or format), you will see an error message directly on the module. - You will also find some additional actions that you can take, which include replacing the image, cropping the image, or removing the image. - Use the preview button to see how your banners will appear instantly as you edit. - You can also scroll down to add more banners for the product. You can add up to 10 image banners per product. 4. Review and Submit - Confirm all banners are valid and meet your quality standards. Then click on Upload. - Once the banner is uploaded you will see the status will show as Ready. Now click on Confirm and Submit to send the content for review. Technical Requirements To ensure your banners pass the validation and quality check, they must meet these criteria: Feature Requirement Recommended Width 2400px (Min: 1800px, Max: 3600px) Height Dynamic Formats JPEG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF File Size Up to 10 MB per image Image Quality Must be clear, not blurry or corrupted Tracking Your Quality Check (QC) Status After submission, your content enters a review process. You can track the status in Seller Lab: Pending QC: The content is currently being reviewed. Approved: The content is live on the site. Rejected: The content did not meet requirements and must be edited. Tips for Success Visual Balance: Avoid using too much text inside images; let the visuals do the talking. Mobile-Friendly: Ensure your banners look good on both desktop and mobile screens. Lifestyle Imagery: Use high-resolution lifestyle images to show the product in a real-world context. Flow: Maintain a clear top-to-bottom story flow for the customer. That's It! Got any more questions? Contact us on seller@noon.com .
