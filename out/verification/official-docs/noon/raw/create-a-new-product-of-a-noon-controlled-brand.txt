Authorized resellers and distributors of noon-controlled brands can now easily list new products on Seller Lab. Here’s a step-by-step process for creating and listing these products, whether individually or in bulk. Overview of the Product Listing Process for noon-Controlled Brands Select Product Creation Type : Begin with the single or bulk product creation workflow. Access Brand Catalog : Go to Seller Lab > Catalog > Brand Catalog and search for your product. Match or Create SKU : If a matching SKU exists, map to it. If not, Create a new noon SKU. How to Identify noon-Controlled Brand Products The product creation process begins the same way for all items. Once you select your product’s category and brand, the workflow may change if the brand is controlled by noon. You’ll receive a prompt if the selected brand is noon-controlled. (This brand is gated. For faster listing, check if your product matches an existing NSKU and list against it. Browse noon Catalog ) Step-by-Step Process for Listing a noon-Controlled Brand Product Create the SKU. - Navigate from Seller Lab - noon.com (Seller Lab > Catalog > My Catalog) and click on Add Products → Create Single Product in the top right corner. Add Title and the Brand - Start by giving your Seller SKU a proper title. Then select the brand that you want to list your SKUs with. Once you select the noon-controlled brand you will see an option to browse the noon catalog for the similar SKU Scenario 1: Check if the SKU exists in noon catalog - You will be redirected to the noon catalog where you can browse if a similar SKU exists in the noon catalog. If you find a similar item click on View more to know the detailed specification of the item. - You will see all the specifications of the product, and you can click on Generate to create a PSKU for your listing and click on Save to proceed. - Once the SKU is created, you will see the hyperlink for the SKU to update your offer details. - In the offer details page you will be requested to update the Pricing information, Stock details, Barcode information and Warranty details. Once all these details are updated you can click on Save to submit your SKU for approval. - Once the SKU is saved, it will be visible in your My Catalog section. You can search for it in the search bar. Scenario 2: Proceed to creating your own content. - You can proceed to create your content by clicking create and continue option during the SKU creation steps. Note: You will notice a disclaimer mentioning that you will be requested for additional documents as this brand is gated - When you proceed to continue, you will be asked to provide the Product content and Offer details. - Once you have filled in the details and click on Submit, you will be redirected to the content page for this SKU. Here you will notice a notification stating that this brand is gated and you can either map it against and existing noon SKU or request noon to create a new noon SKU using your content. Click on this option. - In the next step, you are requested to fill in all the mandatory fields, which are in red, to complete the content to be submitted to noon content team to create a new noon SKU. - Once you will fill all the details, just scroll down at the bottom and you will see an option to Create New noon SKU . Click on this button and your content will be submitted for review to the noon content team. That's it! Got any more questions? Contact us at seller@noon.com
