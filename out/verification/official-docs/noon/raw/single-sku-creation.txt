The cataloging process is designed to help you quickly create your products at noon and have them go live within 3 business days. It gives sellers control and ownership over the content of their products and allows for flexibility in the way SKUs are created. The cataloging process at noon distinguishes between a noon SKU and a Seller SKU. SKU Creation Steps We have updated our website to make it faster and easier for you to add products. Follow these simple steps to create your SKU: Step 1: Product Identity - Log in to the Seller Lab, then click on Catalog in the navigation menu. Then select Add Product . - You will notice the different methods by which you can list your products on noon in the next step. Select Single Products. Then describe your product in a couple of words and upload an image of the item you want to list. If you scroll further down, you will see two options. To fill the content/ details manually To use the AI-generated content. Select the option that best suits your requirements. Generating the Product identity using AI. When you click the Generate Listing button, the system will create the title in both English and Arabic, and you will see suggestions. You can review the title and then approve or reject the suggestions. You also need to enter the PSKU and brand for the listing. Note: The brand and category selection is important for the SKU to go live. You can regenerate the content using the AI generation option a maximum of 3 times at each step. You can further scroll down and select the product category suggested by AI. Then click on Create and Continue to proceed to step 2 - Product Content. Filling the Product identity manually. If you click on Enter details manually , you will be requested to fill in the following information: Titles : Provide the product name in both English and Arabic . Partner SKU : Enter your own internal SKU code or click the Generate icon to have the system create one for you. Brand : Select the brand from the dropdown. If unbranded, check the “ This product does not have a brand name ” box. - Scroll down to finalize the media and classification for your SKU: Product Images: Upload 1 to 9 high-quality images. Product Category: Click Generate Product Category to automatically assign the best classification for your item. Minimum Requirements for a Seller SKU to Go Live: - Title: Follow best practices to create an effective product titles. - Images: Upload clear, high-quality images that showcase your product. Learn more about the image requirements here - Select the most accurate category to ensure your product is discoverable: AI-Generated: Choose from the smart suggestions provided based on your product info. Select Manually: Browse and pick the category hierarchy yourself if the AI suggestions don't fit. - Click Create & Continue to proceed to Product Content. Step 2 : Product Content Generating the Product Content using AI. Once you click on Create & Continue in the product identity step, you will see the suggestions for the product content generated by the AI system. You can review all the suggestions, and according to your requirements, you can accept or reject the suggestions. Important: AI-generated content can occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate. Always review the generated titles, descriptions, and attributes for accuracy before submitting — you are responsible for your final listing content. Once you make your selection for the content you can click on Next to update the offer details. Filling the Product content manually. If you are selecting the manual approach, this step is optional, as you will need to complete the content once the SKU is created. You may complete this step or click Skip . If you wish to complete this step you need to update the following details. Department: Select the relevant product department. Feature Bullets: Add key product highlights. Long Description: Enter a detailed product overview. GTIN: Input the Global Trade Item Number if applicable. - If you wish to skip this step, click Skip to continue to the Offer Details . Step 3 : Offer Details The offer details step is the same for both the AI process flow and the manual process flow. You can finalize your listing by providing stock and pricing information. Partner SKU: Enter your unique internal identifier. Price : Set your selling price (inclusive of VAT). Barcode : Provide the manufacturer barcode (EAN/UPC) for tracking. Warehouse & Stock : FBP (Fulfilled by Partner): Select your warehouse and enter current stock levels. FBN (Fulfilled by noon): Skip the stock field; you will send the inventory later via an ASN request. - Click Submit to send your SKU for review. Pro Tip: If your product has variants (like sizes), select the Product with Sizes tab before entering data. Add/Remove Sizes: Click + Add Size to create new rows or use the trash icon to delete unnecessary ones. Apart from the sizes, you need to fill in the other offer details as well. - Your SKU will be created, and it will take 10-15 minutes for the product to reflect in the catalog. Information: If you’re trying to list a product from a noon-controlled brand, you will see a noon catalog pop-up as the next step. Refer to this article on how to list noon-controlled brand products in your catalog. Content Update Once the SKU is created, you can further enhance your content by adding more information about the product. The more content you provide the better are the chances to gain more visibility. Content A well-written product description boosts your listing's credibility and helps customers make informed decisions. Once you complete the content page, our team will review it and either approve or request changes if it doesn't meet Noon’s standards. For more details on content quality, refer to our content creation guidelines. Once added, the fundamental attributes of a product can be edited. If the seller has made a mistake while adding their product content, they can make corrections in content & images without deleting it. Attention - Please do not make any changes in live products. - You can start by updating the basic content. You will see that the Content check status shows as accepted, as it only comprises the title of the item and the Image of the item. Since you filled this in the product content, that content will be reviewed and approved. You can review any missing fields in the basic content based on the attribute identifier as shown in the screenshot and fill in the respective fields - Then come the images for the products. You can upload images of the item from all angles. - Then comes the video section, where you can add a video about the product, showing how to install the product or how to use the item. The next field under the content is the A+ content. Incase you want to show the A+ content for your products you can update it here in this section. Then comes the details content section. Use this section to enrich your listing with specific attributes that help customers make informed buying decisions. Product Detail Attributes: Fill in technical and descriptive fields such as Features, Care Instructions, and Materials. Colour : Specify the exact Colour and Colour Name to ensure your product appears in relevant customer filters. Bilingual Entries : While English is required, adding Arabic (Optional) translations can significantly increase your reach within the region. Once you have updated all the content you also have an option to enhance the content using the AI enhancer option. When you click on it, a pop-up appears from the left side, which shows the suggestions that could help you enhance your content. You can select which enhancements you want to incorporate in your listing and then click on Apply Changes . You will also see the number of suggestions you selected at the bottom of the screen. Offer Once the initial details are saved, use the sub-navigation menu to optimize your SKU: - In the Offer tab, you will see the option to update your offer for noon and other marketplaces like Supermall, Global if you are eligible to sell on that platform. Offer : Review and update your pricing, barcode, and stock levels. - If you further scroll down, you will see the option to update the warranty and the offer note for the SKU. Product Insights Monitor the health and popularity of your listings with these key metrics: Total GMV : Aggregated GMV over the last 30 days for the selected marketplace. Total Sales : Aggregated units sold over the last 30 days for the selected marketplace. GMV Trend for the last 30 days : Daily GMV aggregated for the last 30 days in the selected marketplace. Unit sold for the last 30 days : Daily units sold aggregated for the last 30 days in the selected marketplace. Business Model Filter : Use the dropdown menu to toggle performance data between different business models. Group The Group option under the catalog PDP allows the seller to map multiple Parent SKUs belonging to the same product type and sub-type, having a shared attribute like color or the item pack quantity. You can either link the SKU to an existing group or create a new group by selecting the shared axes of the group. Note: If the SKU is not linked to any group, you will find the option to either link it to an Existing group or create a New group. - If you choose the Link product to a group option, the pop-up will show the eligible group where you can link this SKU. You can select the suitable group and click on Link Group . - If you want to link the SKU to a new group, you can proceed by clicking on Create New Group . - In the pop-up that appears, you will need to provide a Name to the group and select the shared axes for the group. Then click on Next . - In the next step, you are requested to select the similar Parent SKUs that can be a part of this group. You can select from existing SKUs or create a new SKU. Once the SKU is selected, click on Save to add the SKU to the group. Note: The new SKU will have the same categorization as the current SKU. However, the other content details need to be filled in from your side. - The SKUs will be a part of the new group and will be visible as shown in the screenshot below. - You will have additional functions like adding more SKUs, editing the existing group , and other actions like unlinking the SKU from the group or even deleting the group. Size To wrap up your product setup, use the Sizes tab to manage variants for items like apparel or footwear. Add New Variants: Click + Add New Variant to create additional size options for your SKU. Seller Size: Your internal size label. Display Size: The size customers will see on the noon storefront. noon Size: The standardized size used by noon for categorization. Edit or Remove: Use the pencil icon to modify an existing variant or the trash icon to delete it. Save Changes: Ensure you click Save Changes at the top of the page to apply your updates. Some of the common reasons of SKU being Not Live: - Product Active or Product not Active : It means your product has not been approved by our team yet. - Offer is Gated: it means you’re not allowed to sell this product. Reach out to our seller support team for more information. - Stock Check: means that the stock is not available: you have run out of stock, update that information. That's it! Got any more questions?
