FBN fees and product dimension measurement visibility | noon Seller Help Center noon Seller Help Center › Fulfilled by noon (FBN) › FBN fees and product dimension measurement visibility FBN fees and product dimension measurement visibility noon s FBN Fees dashboard provides sellers with clear visibility into product dimensions captured at... noon’s FBN Fees dashboard provides sellers with clear visibility into product dimensions captured at noon fulfillment centers, along with an estimate of the associated FBN fees, including storage fees and FBN outbound fees . This tool allows sellers to monitor their product measurements and fees, ensuring accuracy and offering the ability to report discrepancies. What Are the Benefits of Using the FBN Fees Dashboard? Visibility of Product Dimensions : View the product dimensions measured at noon’s fulfillment center. Estimate Fees : Get an idea of the estimated FBN outbound fees (shipping fees) and storage fees for your products. Report Measurement or Fee Issues : Quickly report discrepancies in product measurements or estimated fees for review and resolution. Note: the dashboard gives the approximate calculation of your FBN fees. This is just an estimate and may vary as per actual SKU measurements. Please refer to the statement report for actual charges. How to Check Product Measurements and FBN Fees? Go to your Seller Lab > click Fulfilled by noon tab > select the FBN Fees Select the Country and search for the PBarcode of your product Note: as of now you can only search by PBarcode. You can either check the PBarcode for all your items from your Inventory dashboard or you can download the export catalog report by selecting your marketplace and live items. You will be able to see the approximate calculation of your FBN fees. Possible Scenarios on the FBN Fees Dashboard A. Product Is Defined, and Dimensions Are Captured For products with measurements already taken at noon’s fulfillment center, you will see the following details: FBN Inventory Check : Go to Inventory > Click Check FBN Inventory to view stock quantity for the specific PBarcode. Fulfillment From : The country where fulfillment happens (fees are based on the country-specific rate card). Visible Information : Product Title, SKU, PBarcode, and Product Image. Size Classification : Product size tier based on dimensions. Refer to noon Help Center for classification details for KSA , UAE , and Egypt . Product Dimensions : Longest Side , Median Side , and Shortest Side in centimeters. Unit Weight : The actual weight of the product, which is used to calculate outbound fees. Fulfillment Fees ( Based on the dimensions, weight, and fulfillment country, seller will see the following fulfillment fees: FBN Outbound Fees : Charged for every unit shipped from noon’s fulfillment center. Storage Fees : Charged monthly based on the product’s dimensions and weight in cubic feet. Note: By default, fees are displayed for one unit, but you can adjust to see the total storage cost for your entire inventory. Check the help center article for details on how these fees are calculated. B. Product Is Defined, but Dimensions Are Not Yet Captured For recently inbounded SKUs where dimensions have not yet been measured: Details such as dimensions , weight , classification , and fees will not be visible. Temporary Outbound Fees : For such SKUs, fees are charged based on the minimum size tier . Refer to the FBN Fees article for region-specific minimum tier details for KSA , UAE , and Egypt . C. Product Is Not Defined If you search for a PBarcode that does not exist in your catalog, the dashboard will display no results . How to Report an Issue with Product Dimensions or Fees If you notice discrepancies in product dimensions or fees, follow these steps to report an issue: Search for the product using the PBarcode . Click on the Report Issue button. Enter the correct measurement or fees you believe are accurate. Submit your report. Once the issue is reported, noon will investigate and update you via email. Note: You can only report issues for products where dimensions have already been measured FBN Fees Dashboard Tabs (Request Statuses) The FBN Fees Dashboard includes three tabs that allow you to track the progress and resolution of your products' remeasurement requests. The following table provides an explanation of the tabs in the dashboard, and the statuses that accompany your disputes at every stage: Tab Name Tab Description Request Status Pending Newly placed requests that are under review, or that have yet to be reviewed. - Pending: requests that haven't been processed yet. - Open: requests that are being processed. Completed Requests that have been resolved and closed. - Closed: the items in the request have been remeasured or solved with the current dimensions. - Rejected: the request is rejected due to one of the below reasons: - Measurements within the 5% tolerance range. - Invalid input. - Fees within the 5% tolerance range. - Recently reevaluated, and the measurements are accurate. Out of stock Requests that couldn't be processed because you no longer have stock stored in noon, and a sample product related to your request is required for measurement and verification. - Open: the request is still open because a sample product isn't available in noon's fulfillment center to be used for remeasurement. Note : to see the product dimensions' measurements for all inventory, you can download the inventory file from the Inventory dashboard .
