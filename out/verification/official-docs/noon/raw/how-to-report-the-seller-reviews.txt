Online customer reviews are an essential part of the e-commerce experience, providing valuable feedback to both sellers and potential customers. However, there may be times when a review violates community guidelines or contains inappropriate or offensive content. In such cases, it is important to know how to report a seller review to maintain the integrity of the platform. How to report the review? By reporting a review posted by the customer, you trigger an alert to noon indicating the content posted is not suitable for the platform or is offensive. Let's discuss the steps to be followed for reporting a review. Step 1: Click on Account health and Report and select Reputation Management Step 2: Click on the Seller Reviews tab, and this will show all customer reviews for your account. Click on the View more button available right next to the review Step 3: This will open a pop-up window that provides more details about the review and comments. Click on the Report button. Step 4: In the pop-up, select the appropriate reason to report the review, then click on Report review button Once the report is successfully submitted, you will get a notification thanking you for flagging the review. Note: Valid customer review reports may result in the removal of comments, but ratings will not be removed. Valid reasons to report a review: There can be many reasons that the seller would want to report a review. In order to simplify we have categorized these reasons into 3 classifications. They are as follows: 1. Language. 2. Personal Information 3. Harmful content. The following table will give you an explanation and an example for each category mentioned above. Reason category Explanation Examples Language In case the review contains some language other than English or Arabic, or contains some obscene wording, it can be reported by the seller. Unsupported or unintelligible language (Ex: Language other than English and Arabic) Profanity Repetitive text, spam, or symbols Fraudulent schemes Promotional content about a noon competitor External link Questions (Not related to the service provided by the seller) Personal Information Any information related to a specific individual or organization posted online without the consent is prohibited and are a subject to be reported. Email Address. Phone number Address Full name, etc. Harmful content Any reviews supporting or promoting any illegal activities or spreading hate are subjected to be reported.
